## Long description

> Shelf is asset management software for teams that share physical equipment: AV and camera gear, laptops, tools, lab instruments, event equipment. Every item gets a QR label. Scan it to see where it is, who has it, and its history.
>
> Teams use Shelf to assign custody, book equipment ahead with conflict checks, group items into kits, track stock that gets used up, set reminders for inspections and service dates, and run physical audits. Shelf runs in any web browser, and the free Shelf Companion app for iPhone and Android handles scanning, audits, custody and bookings in the field.
>
> Pricing is per workspace, not per person: the Team plan includes unlimited users and unlimited assets. There is a free plan for individuals, and the Team plan has a 7-day free trial with no credit card. Shelf is open source under the AGPL, so the code can be inspected or self-hosted.
>
> Shelf is used by universities, production studios, IT teams and field operations. Shelf Asset Management, Inc. is a Delaware company.
