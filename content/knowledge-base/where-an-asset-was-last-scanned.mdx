Every asset page and kit page carries a **Last scan** card: a map and a short table that answer "when was this last in someone's hands, and where", with a **View all scans** button that opens the [full scan history](#the-full-scan-history). Shelf fills both from the scans people make. Since 16 October 2026 that means **every** scan, whichever code was on the label and wherever the scan happened.

![The Last scan card on an asset's overview page, in the right column under the QR code, showing a map, the date and time, coordinates, device, browser, OS, who scanned it and the source, with the View all scans button underneath](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/last-scan-card.webp)

## Where to find it

Open an asset and stay on its **Overview** tab. The card sits in the right column, under the asset's QR code. A kit page has the same card in the same place. The rows are:

- **Date/Time**: when the last scan happened.
- **Coordinates**: where it happened, with a pin on the map above. When the last scan carried no location, the card keeps the location from the newest earlier scan that had one and says *From the scan on* that date underneath, so you can tell how old the pin is.
- **Device**, **Browser** and **OS**: what the scan was made with. A scan from the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app reads, for example, *Apple iPhone*, *Shelf app 1.5.0* and *iOS*.
- **Scanned By**: the person, with their email address. A scan by someone who is not a member of your workspace, for example a visitor who pointed a phone camera at a sticker without signing in, reads *Unknown*.
- **Source**: how the asset was identified. *QR code*, *Barcode* followed by the barcode's value, *SAM ID* followed by the [SAM ID](/knowledge-base/asset-identifiers-qr-id-sam-id-property-id), or *Manual GPS update* when someone used **Update GPS coordinates** (see below).
- **See in Google Maps** opens the coordinates in Google Maps, when there is a location.
- **View all scans** opens the [scan history](#the-full-scan-history): every scan recorded on this asset, not only the last one.

An asset nobody has scanned yet shows *Waiting for first QR code scan* in place of the map.

## Which scans are recorded

| Where the scan happens | Recorded on the card | Note on the asset's Activity tab |
|---|---|---|
| A phone camera on a Shelf QR sticker, which opens the asset in the browser | Yes, with the browser's location when it is allowed | Yes |
| The web **QR Scanner** with **View asset**, for a QR code, a barcode or a SAM ID | Yes | Yes |
| A web scanner list: scanning items into a booking, a kit, a location or a custody hand-over | Yes | No |
| An audit's scan page, on the web or in the Shelf Companion app | Yes | The audit keeps its own record of the scan |
| The Shelf Companion app's scanner | Yes. A QR scan carries the phone's location when you have allowed it; a barcode scanned in the app is recorded without a location | Yes |
| **Update GPS coordinates** from the asset's **Actions** menu | Yes, as *Manual GPS update* | Yes |

A location is attached when the scanning device shares one. The browser asks for permission on the public QR page and on **Update GPS coordinates**; the app asks once, when you first open its scanner. Scans made without a location still update the date, the device and the person on the card, and the pin stays where the newest located scan put it.

Three details worth knowing:

- **Repeats fold together.** If the same person scans the same code on the same asset again from the same scanner within 30 seconds, it counts as one scan. Opening a QR sticker's link always records a new scan.
- **Scans stay with the asset they were made on.** A scan is recorded against the asset or kit the code pointed to at that moment. If you later [move a sticker to another asset](/knowledge-base/fixing-incorrectly-assigned-qr-stickers), the earlier scans stay with the earlier asset. Scans made before 16 October 2026 count for the asset the code was linked to on that day.
- **Unclaimed codes are not recorded.** A sticker that no workspace has claimed yet has no asset to record a scan on.

## The full scan history

The card shows the newest scan. Click **View all scans** under it and a panel slides in from the right with the asset's or kit's whole scan history, newest first. It loads 25 scans at a time; **Load more** at the bottom fetches the next 25, and if a page fails to load the scans already listed stay put with a **Try again** button. The panel is loaded when you open it, so a long history never slows the asset page down.

![The Scan history panel on an asset, listing four scans newest first, each with what was scanned, the date and time, where it was recorded, who scanned it, the location and the device](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/scan-history-sheet.webp)

Each scan is a short block:

- **What was scanned**, as the headline: *QR code*, *Barcode* with the value, *SAM ID* with the ID, or *Manual GPS update*.
- **Where it was recorded**, as a label on the right: *QR link* (a QR code opened through its link: a phone camera on a sticker, or the web QR Scanner), *Web scanner*, *Scanner list*, *Companion app*, *Audit* or *GPS update*. A scan made before 16 October 2026 reads *Not recorded*, because the place it was made was not saved then.
- **When**, in your [date and time preferences](/knowledge-base/date-time-and-timezone-preferences).
- **Scanned by**: the member's name and email, or *Unknown* for someone outside your workspace.
- **Location**: the coordinates as a link that opens Google Maps, or *No location* when the scan carried none. Unlike the card, a scan in the history never borrows an earlier scan's location.
- **Device**: the hardware and the app or browser, for example *Apple iPhone · Shelf app 1.5.0* or *Apple Macintosh · Safari*, or *Unknown device* when the scan did not say.

The history follows the same rule as the card: it lists scans recorded on this asset or kit, in this workspace. Close the panel with the cross, **Esc** or a click outside it; opening it again starts from the newest scan.

## What the Activity tab says

Where the table above says a note is written, the asset's **Activity** tab gets a line such as *performed a scan of the asset QR code*, *performed a scan of the asset barcode* with the value, or *performed a scan of the asset SAM ID* with the ID, attributed to the person who scanned. Scanner lists leave the Activity tab alone: the booking, kit, location or custody change a scanner list leads to is recorded in its own place. An audit keeps its scans on its scan page. A kit's scans are recorded on the kit's card and do not write a note.

## Who sees the card

Owners, Administrators and Managers see the **Last scan** card and its scan history. Self-service and Base users do not; the asset page shows them everything else. The same rule applies to the asset's **Activity** tab, as listed on the [roles page](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).

## Pinning a location without a scan

When an item is where you say it is but nobody has scanned it there, open the asset, click **Actions** and choose **Update GPS coordinates**. The browser asks for permission to read your location, and the card then shows your coordinates with the source *Manual GPS update*. A manual update always writes a new record, even if you did one a moment ago, and it is listed in the scan history under the label *GPS update*.
