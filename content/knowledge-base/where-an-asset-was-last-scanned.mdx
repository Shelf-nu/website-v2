Every asset page and kit page carries a **Last scan** card: a map and a short table that answer "when was this last in someone's hands, and where". Shelf fills it from the scans people make. Since 16 October 2026 that means **every** scan, whichever code was on the label and wherever the scan happened. Until then only QR code scans were saved, so a team that labels with barcodes never saw the card move.

![The Last scan card on an asset's overview page, in the right column under the QR code, showing a map, the date and time, coordinates, device, browser, OS, who scanned it and the source](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/last-scan-card.webp)

## Where to find it

Open an asset and stay on its **Overview** tab. The card sits in the right column, under the asset's QR code. A kit page has the same card in the same place. The rows are:

- **Date/Time**: when the last scan happened.
- **Coordinates**: where it happened, with a pin on the map above. When the last scan carried no location, the card keeps the location from the newest earlier scan that had one and says *From the scan on* that date underneath, so you can tell how old the pin is.
- **Device**, **Browser** and **OS**: what the scan was made with. A scan from the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app reads, for example, *Apple - iPhone*, *Shelf app 1.5.0* and *iOS*.
- **Scanned By**: the person, with their email address. A scan by someone who is not a member of your workspace, for example a visitor who pointed a phone camera at a sticker without signing in, reads *Unknown*.
- **Source**: how the asset was identified. *QR code scan*, *Barcode scan* followed by the barcode's value, *SAM ID scan* followed by the [SAM ID](/knowledge-base/asset-identifiers-qr-id-sam-id-property-id), or *Manually updated* when someone used **Update GPS coordinates** (see below).
- **See in Google Maps** opens the coordinates in Google Maps.

An asset nobody has scanned yet shows *Waiting for first QR code scan* in place of the map.

## Which scans are recorded

| Where the scan happens | Recorded on the card | Note on the asset's Activity tab |
|---|---|---|
| A phone camera on a Shelf QR sticker, which opens the asset in the browser | Yes, with the browser's location when it is allowed | Yes |
| The web **QR Scanner** with **View asset**, for a QR code, a barcode or a SAM ID | Yes | Yes |
| A web scanner list: scanning items into a booking, a kit, a location or a custody hand-over | Yes | No |
| An audit's scan page, on the web or in the Shelf Companion app | Yes | No, the audit keeps its own record |
| The Shelf Companion app's scanner | Yes. A QR scan carries the phone's location when you have allowed it; a barcode scanned in the app is recorded without a location | Yes |
| **Update GPS coordinates** from the asset's **Actions** menu | Yes, as *Manually updated* | Yes |

A location is attached when the scanning device shares one. The browser asks for permission on the public QR page and on **Update GPS coordinates**; the app asks once, when you first open its scanner. Scans made without a location still update the date, the device and the person on the card, and the pin stays where the newest located scan put it.

Three details worth knowing:

- **Repeats fold together.** Scanning the same code of the same asset again within 30 seconds counts as the scan you already made, so a scanner that fires twice does not write two records.
- **Scans stay with the asset they were made on.** A scan is recorded against the asset or kit the code pointed to at that moment. If you later [move a sticker to another asset](/knowledge-base/fixing-incorrectly-assigned-qr-stickers), the earlier scans stay with the earlier asset. Scans made before 16 October 2026 count for the asset the code was linked to on that day.
- **Unclaimed codes are not recorded.** A sticker that no workspace has claimed yet has no asset to record a scan on.

## What the Activity tab says

Where the table above says a note is written, the asset's **Activity** tab gets a line such as *performed a scan of the asset QR code*, *performed a scan of the asset barcode* with the value, or *performed a scan of the asset SAM ID* with the ID, attributed to the person who scanned. Scanner lists and audits leave the Activity tab alone: the booking, kit, location or custody change a scanner list leads to is recorded in its own place, and an audit keeps its scans on its scan page. A kit's scans are recorded on the kit's card and do not write a note.

## Who sees the card

Owners, Administrators and Managers see the **Last scan** card. Self-service and Base users do not; the asset page shows them everything else. The same rule applies to the asset's **Activity** tab, as listed on the [roles page](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).

## Pinning a location without a scan

When an item is where you say it is but nobody has scanned it there, open the asset, click **Actions** and choose **Update GPS coordinates**. The browser asks for permission to read your location, and the card then shows your coordinates with the source *Manually updated*. A manual update always writes a new record, even if you did one a moment ago.
