You signed up, the Team trial is running, and the workspace is empty. The trial lasts 7 days and needs no card, so the only thing that costs you anything is spending the week on the wrong features. This guide gives you a short test plan per use case: what to set up, in which order, and what you should see when it works. Pick the list that matches your team and skip the rest.

If you have not started the trial yet, [the Team trial article](/knowledge-base/free-trial) covers how to activate it.

## Before you start: get into a Team workspace

Every account has a **Personal** workspace. The trial unlocks the Team plan for your account, but the features you are evaluating live in a **Team** workspace, so create one first:

- When the trial starts, Shelf sends you straight to the **New workspace** page. Give it a name and save.
- If you skipped that page, open the menu under your name, choose **Workspaces**, and click **New workspace**.
- Check the workspace name at the top left before you add anything. If it still says your own name, you are in the Personal workspace.

Two things only work in a Team workspace: **bookings** and **inviting other users**. The trial also unlocks **CSV import**, **CSV export**, up to 100 **custom fields** (a free Personal workspace has 3) and importing non-registered members. If you are unsure which workspace you are in, see [Why You Can't Invite Anyone to a Personal Workspace](/knowledge-base/why-you-cant-invite-anyone-to-a-personal-workspace).

**Audits** and **alternative barcodes** are add-ons with their own 7-day trial per workspace. The owner starts each one from inside the app, for example from the unlock page under **Audits**, with no sales contact required.

Shelf also shows a short checklist on the dashboard of a new workspace with links to the first things to set up. It is a fine place to start; the lists below go further.

## Media and production crews

For camera, sound and lighting gear that goes out on shoots and comes back as a set.

1. **Create your Team workspace** and add two or three **locations** such as *Camera cage*, *Studio A* and *Van 1*. Outcome: a location dropdown you can use on every asset. See [Locations vs Categories vs Tags](/knowledge-base/locations-vs-categories-vs-tags).
2. **Import your gear list.** Go to **Assets**, click **Import**, download the CSV template and fill it from your spreadsheet. Categories and locations are created on the fly from the file. Outcome: your real inventory in Shelf in one go. See the [CSV import guide](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide); the **Copy prompt** box there lets your own AI assistant reshape your spreadsheet into Shelf's format.
3. **Print a few QR labels.** Select five or six assets in the Assets list, open **Actions** and choose **Download QR Codes**. Print the zip on square labels and stick them on. Outcome: a label that opens the right asset when you scan it with your phone camera. See [Using Shelf QR Codes with Avery Label Sheets](/knowledge-base/using-shelf-qr-codes-with-avery-label-sheets).
4. **Build a kit.** Go to **Kits**, click **New kit**, name it after a real package such as *Interview kit* and use **Manage assets** to add the camera body, lens, lav mics and tripod. Outcome: one QR code and one custody record for the whole package. See [Kits](/knowledge-base/kits).
5. **Book the kit for a shoot.** Go to **Bookings**, create a booking with a name, start and end date and a custodian, add the kit, then **Reserve** it. On the shoot day use **Check-Out**; afterwards **Check-In**. Outcome: the kit shows as unavailable for the dates, and the booking moves from Reserved to Ongoing to Complete. See [How to Create a Booking](/knowledge-base/how-to-create-a-booking) and [Progressive Checkout](/knowledge-base/progressive-checkout-of-bookings) for handing items out one at a time.
6. **Check the Availability view** on the Assets page with two overlapping bookings. Outcome: a calendar bar per asset so you can see what is free for the next job. See [Availability View: Complete Guide](/knowledge-base/availability-view-complete-guide).
7. **Install Shelf Companion** on an iPhone or Android phone. It is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765639874) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shelf.companion), and signs in with your Shelf account. Scan a label and check out a booking from the phone. See [Getting Started with Shelf Companion](/knowledge-base/shelf-ios-companion-getting-started).
8. **Invite two colleagues.** Under **Settings**, open **Team** and invite one as **Self service** and one as **Administrator**. Outcome: the self-service user can only book and hold gear, the admin can do what you can. See [User Roles and Their Permissions](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).

## Universities and equipment rooms

For a checkout desk that lends equipment to students and staff for a class, a project or a semester.

1. **Create your Team workspace** and add **categories** such as *Cameras*, *Audio* and *Laptops*. Outcome: filters that match how your desk already sorts gear. See [Using Categories](/knowledge-base/using-categories-to-organize-your-asset-inventory).
2. **Import your inventory** from the CSV template under **Assets**, then **Import**. Add a `cf:Serial number` column so serials land in a custom field. Outcome: every item searchable by name or serial. See the [CSV import guide](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide).
3. **Add your borrowers as non-registered members.** Under **Settings**, open **Team** and add a few students by name. They cannot log in, but you can assign gear to them. Outcome: a borrower list without giving out accounts. See [Onboarding Your Team Members](/knowledge-base/onboarding-your-team-members).
4. **Hand something out with custody.** Open an asset, choose **Assign custody** and pick a student. Outcome: the asset shows who has it with no return date, which suits semester-long loans. Release it when it comes back. See [Custody for Long-Term Lend-Outs](/knowledge-base/custody-feature-for-long-term-equipment-lend-outs).
5. **Book a class set.** Create a booking for next week's class, add ten assets, **Reserve** it, then try to book one of the same assets for the same day in a second booking. Outcome: Shelf blocks the second booking. See [Troubleshooting Booking Conflicts](/knowledge-base/troubleshooting-booking-conflicts).
6. **Print QR labels** for a shelf of items via **Actions**, then **Download QR Codes**, and scan one with the in-app scanner. Outcome: the scanner opens the asset, and the **Assign custody** action in the scanner lets you hand out several items by scanning them in a row. See [Batch Scanning Actions](/knowledge-base/batch-scanning-actions).
7. **Install Shelf Companion** on the phone that lives at the desk. It is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765639874) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shelf.companion). Use it to check a booking in from the return counter. See [Getting Started with Shelf Companion](/knowledge-base/shelf-ios-companion-getting-started).
8. **Invite two colleagues.** Invite a second desk worker as **Administrator** and a lecturer as **Self service**, then sign in as the lecturer and confirm they can book but not edit assets. See [User Roles and Their Permissions](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).

## Field and construction teams

For tools, instruments and safety gear that move between sites and people.

1. **Create your Team workspace** and add your sites as **locations**, with **sub-locations** such as *Site B, Container 2* where it helps. Outcome: a location tree that mirrors where things physically are. See [Sub-locations](/knowledge-base/sublocations-organize-locations-into-hierarchies).
2. **Import your tool register** from the CSV template under **Assets**, then **Import**, with a location column filled in. Outcome: every tool already sitting in the right site. See the [CSV import guide](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide).
3. **Print QR labels** and attach them to a handful of tools. Use the **Actions** menu in the Assets list, then **Download QR Codes**. Outcome: scanning the label on site opens the tool's record with its current holder and location. See [Scanning an Asset](/knowledge-base/scanning-an-asset).
4. **Hand tools to a crew member with custody.** Open a tool, choose **Assign custody** and pick the person. Outcome: the asset shows its holder, and the hand-over is written to the asset's activity log with a timestamp. See [Custody for Long-Term Lend-Outs](/knowledge-base/custody-feature-for-long-term-equipment-lend-outs).
5. **Move gear between sites by scanning.** Open the scanner, switch its action to **Update location**, scan several tools and pick the new site. Outcome: all of them show the new location in one step. See [Batch Scanning Actions](/knowledge-base/batch-scanning-actions).
6. **Track consumables by count.** Add a quantity-tracked asset such as *Safety gloves*, set its quantity and a **Min quantity**, then assign some to a person. Outcome: the available count drops, and a low-stock alert fires when it reaches the minimum. See [Quantity-Tracked Assets and Consumables](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables).
7. **Set a reminder** on an instrument for its next calibration date. Outcome: an email when the date comes round. See [Asset Reminders](/knowledge-base/asset-reminders).
8. **Install Shelf Companion** on the site lead's phone, free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765639874) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shelf.companion), and **invite two colleagues**: a site lead as **Manager** and a crew member as **Base**. Outcome: the Manager can run custody and bookings for everyone, and the crew member can browse and request gear but not change it. See [User Roles and Their Permissions](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).

## IT and office equipment

For laptops, monitors, phones and peripherals assigned to employees.

1. **Create your Team workspace** and add **custom fields** such as *Serial number* (text), *Warranty end* (date) and *Condition* (dropdown). Outcome: the fields appear on every asset form. See [Custom Field Types](/knowledge-base/custom-field-types-in-shelf).
2. **Import your device list** from the CSV template under **Assets**, then **Import**. Put the custom field values in columns with the `cf:` prefix as the template shows. Outcome: every device with its serial and warranty date. See the [CSV import guide](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide).
3. **Keep your existing asset tags.** If devices already carry barcode stickers, start the **Alternative Barcodes** trial and put the sticker values in the import file. Outcome: scanning an old sticker opens the device in Shelf. See [Alternative Barcodes](/knowledge-base/alternative-barcodes).
4. **Assign devices to employees with custody.** Add colleagues as non-registered members under **Settings**, then **Team**, and give each a laptop via **Assign custody**. Outcome: each employee's page lists their equipment. See [Custody for Long-Term Lend-Outs](/knowledge-base/custody-feature-for-long-term-equipment-lend-outs).
5. **Retire a device.** Archive an old laptop and check that it drops out of the Assets list but keeps its history, then reinstate it. Outcome: a clean list without deleting anything. See [Archive and Reinstate Assets](/knowledge-base/archive-and-reinstate-assets).
6. **Run a quick audit.** Start the Audits trial from the **Audits** page, create a session for one room, and scan what is there. Outcome: a found, missing and unexpected list plus a PDF report. See [Run Your First Audit](/knowledge-base/run-your-first-audit).
7. **Install Shelf Companion**, free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6765639874) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.shelf.companion), and run the audit scan from the phone instead of a laptop. See [Getting Started with Shelf Companion](/knowledge-base/shelf-ios-companion-getting-started).
8. **Invite two colleagues** from **Settings**, then **Team**: a helpdesk colleague as **Administrator** and an employee as **Base**. Outcome: the Base user sees the devices they hold and can request more, but cannot edit anything. Then **export** the asset list to CSV to check the data comes back out. See [Exporting Workspace Data](/knowledge-base/export-exporting-workspace-data-from-shelf).

## At the end of the week

- Open the **Dashboard** and the **Reports** page to see what a week of real use looks like. See [Getting Started with Reports](/knowledge-base/getting-started-with-reports).
- Decide what you still need to see. If it is not covered above, [write to support](/contact) with your use case and we will point you to it or tell you plainly if Shelf does not do it.
- When the trial ends the Team workspace pauses, and your data stays. You can add a payment method from **Subscriptions** to continue, as described in [How to Upgrade to Team](/knowledge-base/how-to-upgrade-to-team).
