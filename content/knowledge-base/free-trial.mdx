## Overview

If you want to try out team asset management features, a trial provides an excellent starting point. You can explore Shelf at your own pace by importing inventory or creating items individually. The trial enables testing of the bookings module and customizing fields without restrictions, plus inviting co-administrators to your workspace.

## How to Activate a Team Workspace Trial

### New to Shelf: start from the pricing page

1. On the [pricing page](/pricing), click **Start Free Trial** on the Team plan.
2. The signup page confirms your choice under the heading: *"You're starting a Team trial. 7 days, no credit card."* Create your account.
3. Enter the code from your email and finish onboarding.
4. Shelf skips the Personal-or-Team question and opens the plan page with the Team trial first. Start the trial there.

Changed your mind? The link under the plans, **Continue with a free Personal workspace instead**, does the same as choosing Personal. You can start the Team trial later from **Subscriptions**.

### Already have an account

1. Open the menu under your name and choose **Subscriptions**
2. In the Team plan column, select the button to activate a trial, which directs you to a confirmation page

## Key Trial Details

### Duration and Support

The Team trial lasts 7 days. At trial's end, a Shelf team member will contact you for feedback and discuss whether a subscription suits your business.

### Payment Requirements

The trial does not require any payment method. Your team workspace will pause when the trial concludes — you will have the option to add payment details and continue.

**If you already have a payment method on file:** When starting a trial with a payment method saved in your account, Shelf requires you to check a consent checkbox confirming that you understand your payment method will be automatically charged when the trial ends. This prevents unexpected charges. You can cancel before the trial ends from **Subscriptions** to avoid being billed.

### One Team trial per account

The Team trial belongs to your account, not to a workspace. Once you have used it, the Team plan column on **Subscriptions** offers **Upgrade to Team** only, with no trial button.

This also applies when a workspace changes hands. If you [take over a workspace](/knowledge-base/transfer-workspace-ownership) from an owner who already used their Team trial, your account counts as having used it too. The workspace already holds their assets, people and history, so it does not get a second trial through a new owner.

### Getting Started

All that is needed is a Shelf account. For assistance, contact the support team at [support@shelf.nu](mailto:support@shelf.nu).

## Add-on Trials: Audits and Barcodes

In addition to the Team plan trial, Shelf offers 7-day free trials for paid add-ons:

- **Audits add-on**: Run physical inventory audits with QR scanning, found/missing/unexpected detection, and a downloadable PDF receipt
- **Barcodes add-on**: Scan and manage Code128, Code39, Data Matrix, and EAN-13 barcodes alongside Shelf QR codes

Both add-on trials can be activated directly from within your workspace — no sales contact required. Workspace owners start the trial from the relevant section (for example, navigating to the Audits area shows an unlock page with trial and subscription options).

The same auto-charge consent applies: if you have a payment method on file when starting an add-on trial, you will need to check a consent box before the trial button becomes active. You'll also receive email reminders at 3 days and 1 day before the trial ends if auto-charge applies to your account.

**Each add-on trial can be used once per workspace.** Audits and Barcodes have a trial each, so taking one does not spend the other, but neither can be started a second time on the same workspace. Once a workspace has used its Audits trial, the unlock page offers the paid subscription rather than the trial button. If you are still evaluating and need more time, [contact support](mailto:support@shelf.nu) rather than starting over: a trial cannot be reset from within the app.
