You can change a team member's role in place from your workspace settings. The change takes effect immediately, and the user keeps their account, their history, and their place in the workspace.

## Changing a Role

1. Go to **Workspace settings → Team → Users**
2. Find the user and click the **Actions** menu on their row
3. Choose **Change role**
4. Pick the new role: **Administrator**, **Self service**, or **Base**
5. Click **Change role**

That's it. The user's permissions update straight away, and they don't need to accept anything or sign in again.

## Lowering a Role

Moving someone **down** a level, for example from Administrator to Self service, is treated as a demotion. Because the lower roles can't own workspace-wide records, the dialog asks where those records should go before it lets you continue.

Shelf shows you exactly what will move:

- Assets, kits, categories, tags, locations, custom fields, asset reminders, and images the user created
- **Bookings created for others**, meaning bookings this user set up with someone else as the custodian

Pick a **Transfer entities to** recipient from the dropdown. The workspace owner is preselected, and any other member of the workspace can be chosen instead. If the user owns nothing, the dialog says there is nothing to transfer and you can go ahead.

> **The user keeps their own bookings.** Only bookings they created for a different custodian move to the recipient. Their own reservations and their own drafts stay with them and remain visible to them after the change.

Raising a role, for example from Base to Administrator, transfers nothing. Nothing needs to move, so there is no extra step.

## Who Can Change a Role

- **Administrators and the workspace owner** can change roles.
- **You cannot change your own role.** Ask another administrator or the owner.
- **Only the workspace owner can change an Administrator's role.** One administrator cannot demote another.
- **Users managed through SSO** cannot be changed here. Their role comes from the group mapping in your identity provider, so make the change there instead. See [SSO with Google Workspace](/knowledge-base/sso-with-google-workspace).

## Revoking Access Instead

**Revoke access**, in the same **Actions** menu, is a different action: it removes the person from the workspace rather than adjusting what they can do. They become a Non-Registered Member (NRM), which keeps all their assets, custody records, and history intact, and you can invite them back later from the Non-Registered Members section.

Use **Change role** when someone stays on the team with different permissions, and **Revoke access** when they are leaving.
