Reports turn the data Shelf already captures — bookings, custody, assets — into answers you can read, filter, and export. This guide walks through running your first report.

## Open the Reports Index

In the sidebar, click **Reports**. The index lists every available report grouped by category:

- **Bookings** — Booking Compliance, Top Booked Assets, Top Booked Kits, Monthly Booking Trends, Overdue Items
- **Assets** — Asset Inventory, Asset Activity Summary, Asset Utilization, Idle Assets, Asset Distribution
- **Custody** — Custody Snapshot

Each card describes what the report shows. Click any enabled card to run it.

![Reports index in Shelf showing the grid of reports grouped by category](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/reports-index.webp)

## Pick a Timeframe

Time-based reports — Booking Compliance, Top Booked Assets, Top Booked Kits, Monthly Booking Trends, Asset Activity Summary, Asset Utilization, Idle Assets — include a **timeframe picker** at the top of the page.

1. Click the timeframe picker.
2. Choose a preset — **Today**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default), **Last 90 days**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **This year**, **All time**, or define a custom range.
3. The report re-runs against the new timeframe.

Snapshot reports — **Asset Inventory**, **Custody Snapshot**, **Overdue Items** — always reflect the current state of the workspace, so they do not show a timeframe picker.

## Apply Filters

Each report exposes filters that match the underlying data:

| Report | Filters |
|---|---|
| Booking Compliance | Status, Custodian, Location |
| Top Booked Assets | Category, Location |
| Top Booked Kits | Category, Location |
| Monthly Booking Trends | Category, Location |
| Overdue Items | Custodian, Location |
| Asset Inventory | Category, Location, Status |
| Asset Activity Summary | Asset, Category |
| Asset Utilization | Category, Location |
| Idle Assets | Category, Location |
| Asset Distribution | — |
| Custody Snapshot | Team Member, Location |

Filters are written into the URL, so you can **bookmark or share** a specific view of a report. A link to "Idle Assets in the Amsterdam warehouse, last 90 days" works for everyone with access to the workspace.

## Read the Result

Reports are laid out in three layers:

1. **KPI cards** at the top — headline numbers like total bookings, on-time rate, or assets outstanding.
2. **Charts** in the middle — trends, distributions, or compliance breakdowns.
3. **Tables** at the bottom — the underlying records that make up the numbers.

Compliance and utilization reports use color to flag entries that need attention — late bookings, idle assets that have not moved in months, or custodians with overdue items.

## How Booking Compliance Decides On Time or Late

Booking Compliance answers one question: was the deadline the custodian agreed to honoured? The report says so in its own words, in the **How it's calculated** line under the headline rate.

![The Booking Compliance report showing the On-Time Return Rate, the How it's calculated explainer, and the Return Status column with late durations](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/booking-compliance-calculation.webp)

Four rules govern every number on the page.

**It measures against the planned end date, not the current one.** A booking's planned period is recorded when the booking is created, still moves with any edit made while it is a draft, and is fixed from the moment it is reserved. Extending a booking, or checking it in early, moves the live dates and leaves the planned dates alone. So a booking that was due Friday and came back Monday is a late return, whether or not someone extended it to Monday first.

**A return inside 15 minutes of the scheduled end is on time.** That grace absorbs the walk back to the counter and the scan at the desk, so a queue at the cage does not read as a compliance failure.

**Only finished or overdue bookings are counted.** **Complete** and **Archived** bookings are measured against their check-in time. **Overdue** bookings are always late, by definition. Drafts, reservations, bookings currently **Ongoing** and cancelled bookings are left out: they have not run yet, are still inside their window, or never happened. A reservation archived without ever being checked out is excluded too, so it neither helps nor hurts your rate. See [what archiving means for reports](/knowledge-base/archiving-bookings#what-archiving-means-for-reports).

**A booking is counted in the period its planned end falls in.** Because the planned end never moves, a booking stays in the same period for its whole life instead of sliding into the next month when someone extends it.

The **Return Status** column shows the verdict per booking: **On time**, or how far past the deadline it came back, as `1d 8h late`.

### Why Booking Compliance and Overdue Items Can Disagree

The two reports ask different questions, so for an extended booking they will legitimately give different answers.

| | Booking Compliance | Overdue Items |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Was the agreed plan honoured? | What is late right now? |
| Measures against | The planned end date | The current end date |
| An extended booking | Still measured against the original deadline | Measured against the new deadline |

An overdue booking extended to next week disappears from Overdue Items straight away, because it is no longer late against its current deadline. It still counts as a late return in Booking Compliance once it comes back, because the deadline it was agreed to has already passed. Extending a booking is a way to keep operations moving, not a way to clear a late return from the record.

## How Money Is Counted

Several reports put a value on things: Asset Distribution, Overdue Items, Asset Inventory and Custody Snapshot. Shelf stores one **value per unit** on an asset, and every money figure in a report is that per-unit value multiplied by the quantity the figure is actually about.

Which quantity that is depends on what the number is answering:

| Where you see it | What it multiplies by |
|---|---|
| Overdue Items, value at risk | The units **booked** on the overdue booking |
| Asset Distribution, category and status buckets | The asset's **workspace stock** |
| Asset Distribution, location buckets | The units **placed at that location** |
| Asset Inventory | The asset's **workspace stock** |
| Custody Snapshot | The units **that custodian holds** |

For an individually tracked asset all of these are one, so its value appears once and the distinction never comes up. For [quantity-tracked stock](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) it is the difference between a report that adds up and one that does not: a $100 item with 5 units booked is $500 of value at risk, not $100.

A headline figure and the breakdown underneath it now always agree. A pool split across two locations has its value split with it, and any units you have not placed anywhere are carried under **No Location**, so the location buckets add up to the same total as the headline rather than quietly losing the remainder.

## Export the Result

Every report supports **CSV export**. Click **Export** at the top right of any report to download the data shown.

Two of those files spell the value arithmetic out rather than leaving you to redo it in a spreadsheet:

- **Asset Inventory** carries **Quantity**, **Unit Value** and **Total Value**.
- **Custody Snapshot** carries **Units Held**, **Unit Value** and **Total Value**.

The PDF versions total the same way, so a printed report and the CSV behind it never disagree.

Report CSVs are written as UTF-8 with a byte order mark, so category, location and custodian names in any alphabet open correctly in Excel without an import wizard. See [How the file is encoded](/knowledge-base/export-exporting-workspace-data-from-shelf#how-the-file-is-encoded).

Reports with charts also support **PDF download** — Shelf renders the charts and tables into a print-ready document. PDF download is available for chart-based reports (Booking Compliance, Top Booked Assets, Monthly Booking Trends, Asset Activity Summary, Asset Utilization, Asset Distribution); reports without a chart — Asset Inventory, Idle Assets, Overdue Items, Custody Snapshot, and Top Booked Kits — export to CSV. PDFs are useful for board packs, audit trails, and sharing with people outside the workspace.

## Who Can Run Reports

**Reports are for Administrators and Owners.** A report reads across every booking, custodian and asset value in the workspace at once, which is a wider question than any single page answers, so the whole area is theirs.

**Reports** has never appeared in the sidebar for **Self-service** and **Base** users. It is now refused by direct link as well: the report pages, the CSV export and the PDF export all turn those two roles away, so a bookmarked or forwarded report URL no longer opens for them. Neither of the workspace [visibility toggles](/knowledge-base/configure-what-self-service-and-base-users-can-see) changes this, and reports remain included on every plan.

What each report shows is still scoped to the workspace it was run in.

## Common Reports by Use Case

- **"What's overdue right now?"** → Overdue Items
- **"Which assets are getting the most use?"** → Top Booked Assets, Asset Utilization
- **"Which kits get booked most often?"** → Top Booked Kits
- **"What can we redistribute or retire?"** → Idle Assets, Asset Distribution
- **"How well are we returning bookings on time?"** → Booking Compliance
- **"Who has what right now?"** → Custody Snapshot
- **"What's the inventory state for an audit?"** → Asset Inventory
- **"How are bookings trending month over month?"** → Monthly Booking Trends
