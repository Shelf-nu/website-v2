## How Early Check-in/Check-out Works


![Early check-in and check-out warning dialog in Shelf bookings](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/early-checkin-1.webp)
### Early Check-out

When checking out assets before the scheduled start date:

- A warning message appears notifying you that the booking start date will be adjusted to the current time
- You can choose to:
  - Cancel the action
  - Adjust the date (updating the booking start date to now)
  - Proceed without adjusting dates

### Quick Check-out Asks First

Since 22 October 2026 the **Check out** button on a booking, and **Check out remaining** on one that is already **Ongoing** or **Overdue**, never check out in one click. When the check-out is on time, they open a **Check out booking** dialog that names the booking: *"This checks out every asset of Studio Shoot and starts the booking."*, or for the rest of a booking *"This checks out the remaining assets of Studio Shoot."* **Check out** goes ahead; **Cancel** leaves the booking as it is. An early check-out opens the warning above instead, so you are asked once, not twice. Units still to assign and the **From location** question for pooled stock keep their own sections. Flows that already end in a confirmation of their own, **Check out selected items**, **Scan to check out** and **Fulfil reservations & check out**, do not add this dialog.

### Quick Check-in Asks First

Since 19 October 2026 **Quick check-in**, the whole-booking return in the **Check-in** menu, opens a **Check in booking** dialog before anything is returned. It names the booking and says what will happen: *"This checks in every asset of Studio Shoot and completes the booking."* **Check in** completes the booking; **Cancel** leaves it as it is. A [partial check-in](/knowledge-base/partial-check-ins-efficiently-handle-incomplete-returns), from **Check in selected items** or the scanner, already confirms the items you picked, and only adds this dialog, naming *N assets*, when it checks in the last items more than 15 minutes before the end date.

### Early Check-in

When checking in assets more than 15 minutes before the scheduled end date, the same dialog adds the early-return question:

- It says the end date can be moved to now, and that **Don't Adjust Date** keeps the original end date
- Options include:
  - **Cancel**
  - **Adjust Date** (updating the booking end date to now)
  - **Don't Adjust Date** (keep the original end date)

If the current time is still before the booking's **start** date, the end date cannot be moved to a time before the start, so the dialog says so and checks the booking in without changing its dates.

## The Planned Period and the Actual Period

Every booking carries two periods, and adjusting the dates at check-out or check-in never overwrites the first one.

| | What it holds | When it is written |
|---|---|---|
| **Planned period** | The dates that were agreed | Creating the booking, and editing it while it is still a draft. Fixed once it is reserved |
| **Actual period** | The dates the booking really ran | Extending the booking, checking in a booking that is **Overdue**, and the date adjustments offered at early check-out and early check-in |

The planned period is frozen from the moment the booking is reserved. Nothing that happens afterwards moves it: not an early check-in, not an early check-out, and not an [extension](/knowledge-base/extending-booking-end-dates-in-shelf).

One path onto the actual end date is not a choice anyone makes. **Checking in a booking that is already Overdue always moves its end date to the moment it came back**, with no dialog and no option to decline, because the booking has to record when the gear actually returned. That is the ordinary way an actual end date comes to differ from a planned one, and it is why the [Booking Compliance report](/knowledge-base/getting-started-with-reports#how-booking-compliance-decides-on-time-or-late) reads the planned end rather than the live one. The early check-out and early check-in adjustments below are the ones you are asked about.

### Where to Read Both Periods

- **The booking CSV export** carries four date columns: **Planned start date**, **Planned end date**, **Actual start date** and **Actual end date**. See [exporting booking information](/knowledge-base/bookings-how-to-export-booking-information-and-assets-checked-out).
- **The booking's PDF overview** adds an **Original period** row underneath the booking period, and only when the two differ.

The booking page itself shows the actual period. If you need the agreed dates alongside it, use the PDF overview or the CSV export.

## Important Notes

- While a booking is still a draft, editing its dates moves both periods together. The planned period only becomes fixed once the booking is reserved.
- Scheduled reminders and overdue notifications follow the actual dates, not the planned ones. Adjust the end date at check-in and the reminders follow it.
- The [Booking Compliance report](/knowledge-base/getting-started-with-reports#how-booking-compliance-decides-on-time-or-late) measures returns against the **planned** end date, so an early return is on time and a booking extended past its deadline is still recorded as a late return. Overdue Items uses the actual end date, because it answers what is late right now.

## Use Cases

This feature helps when:

- Equipment is no longer needed for the full originally scheduled period
- Equipment must be returned early for urgent requests
- Tracking actual versus planned usage for reporting and analytics

The ability to track both original and adjusted periods provides transparency for resource planning and utilization analysis.
