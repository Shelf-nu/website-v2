## What the Check-in Receipt Is

The [booking checklist PDF](/knowledge-base/how-to-generate-a-pdf-overview-of-your-reserved-assets-bookings) is a list of work to do: what to pack, with a tick box on every row. It looks the same before and after the gear comes back, so it cannot tell anyone what was actually returned.

The **check-in receipt** is the other half. It is a record of what happened on the way back, printed from the check-ins Shelf already stored. Hand it to the person returning the gear, attach it to a damage or loss claim, or file it with the job.

![The check-in receipt preview for a completed booking, with the All items returned stamp, the booking's planned period, check-out and return times, and one row per item marked Returned](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/booking-checkin-receipt-preview.webp)

## How to Print It

1. Open the booking.
2. Open the **Actions** menu in the top right.
3. Choose **Generate check-in receipt**. It sits right under **Generate overview PDF**.
4. The receipt opens as a preview. Click **Download PDF** to save or print it.

![The booking Actions menu with Generate check-in receipt highlighted, directly under Generate overview PDF](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/booking-checkin-receipt-menu.webp)

### When the entry is available

You do not have to wait for the booking to finish. The entry works as soon as something went out and something has come back, so a partial return, the case a printed record is most often wanted for, can be printed while the rest is still out.

Until then the entry is greyed out and says why:

- **"This booking was never checked out."** Nothing on the booking left the shelf. A reserved booking that was archived without going out lands here too, because a return document for gear that never moved would be false.
- **"Nothing has been checked in yet."** The booking is out, but nothing is back.

## What the Receipt Shows

### The stamp

The bordered stamp in the top right is the one place on the sheet that states completeness. It reads one of these:

- **All items returned**: every unit that went out came back.
- **All items accounted for**: nothing is outstanding, but some units were marked consumed, lost or damaged rather than returned.
- **Partial return · N still out**: some units came back and N are still out.
- **Nothing returned · N still out**: units are still out and none came back. Some may have been written off.

The outstanding count is in units. An individually tracked item counts as 1, and a [quantity-tracked](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) line counts every unit it still owes.

### The booking details

- **Booking** and **Custodian**.
- **Planned period**: the dates the booking was made for. Checking a booking out early, or checking it in after it ran late, can change the dates shown on the booking page. The receipt still prints the dates that were agreed.
- **Checked out**, with the name of the person who checked it out when Shelf recorded one.
- **Returned**, the moment the return was recorded, plus how it compares to the planned end: *on time*, *early*, or for example *4 days 22 hours after the planned end*. A booking closed entirely through consumed, lost or damaged units labels this row **Accounted for** instead, because nothing was returned.
- **Checked in by**, every person who received a return.
- The booking's description and tags, when it has them.

### One row per booked item

Each row gives the item's name, quantity, kit, and code, then what happened to it:

- An individually tracked item reads **Returned**, **Still out**, or **Never checked out**.
- A quantity-tracked line lists its units by outcome, for example **5 returned** and **1 damaged**, or **3 returned** and **3 still out** while a return is still in progress.

**Checked in on** and **Checked in by** are filled in from what Shelf recorded at check-in. On older bookings the **Checked in by** column can be empty, because Shelf did not always record who checked each item in. The receipt leaves it blank rather than filling in the custodian or the person printing.

The **Code** column prints the code your workspace chose under **Settings → General ▸ Preferred display code**, as text, the same code the checklist prints. The receipt never prints a QR image, because it is a record rather than a sheet to scan from.

A row for an item that was taken out of its kit after the booking was made says so in the Kit cell: *Removed from kit — kept as a record of what was booked*.

### Totals and signatures

Under the table, a ledger counts **Items booked**, **Units sent out**, **Returned**, **Consumed**, **Lost**, **Damaged**, and **Still out**. Every line prints, zeros included, because a zero is evidence that nothing was lost.

At the foot of the sheet are two signature lines for name, signature and date. When units came back they read **Returned by (custodian)** and **Received by**. When nothing was returned they read **Confirmed by (custodian)** and **Recorded by**, so nobody is asked to sign for a return that did not happen. The signatures are on paper only; Shelf does not collect them.

The receipt prints no asset values and no prices, so it is safe to hand to a custodian or a client.

## Who Can Print It

- **Owners and Administrators** can print the receipt for any booking in the workspace.
- **Self-service and Base** users can print it for bookings where they are the custodian, the same rule that applies to the checklist PDF.
