## Configuring Custom Fields for Assets

- Go to **Settings**
- Navigate to the **Custom fields** tab
- Click **Add custom field**
- Fill a **name** for the field
- Choose a **field type**
- Specify if the field is **required** or **optional**
- Specify if the field is **active** (Deactivating a field will no longer show it on the asset form and page)
- Fill a **Help text** (This text will function as a help text that is visible when filling the field)


![Custom Fields settings page](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/custom-fields-settings.webp)

### What Active and Inactive Do to Your Asset Index Column

A custom field is not only a box on the asset form. An **active** field also gets its own column on the [advanced asset index](/knowledge-base/advanced-asset-index-complete-guide), and Shelf keeps the two in step for you:

- **Creating an active field** adds its column for everyone in the workspace, switched on, so nobody has to go and find it.
- **Deactivating a field** takes the column away again, from every member's index rather than only from yours.
- **Reactivating it** brings the column back. If your own copy of the column was still there, your visibility and position choices are kept: reactivating is not a reason to reset where you put it.
- **Renaming a field** renames the column and leaves it exactly where you had it.

Creating a field as **inactive** gives it no column at all until you activate it.

## Using Custom Fields

### Using Custom Fields for a New Asset

![Custom fields on an asset detail page](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/custom-fields-on-asset.webp)

1. Go to **Assets**, click **New asset**
2. Fill the required **default** and **custom** fields
3. Click **Save**

### Updating a Custom Field of an Existing Asset

For a quick change to a single field, edit it inline:

1. Go to **Assets**
2. **Select the asset** of which you want to update the field
3. **Hover** the custom field on the asset detail page and click the **pencil** icon
4. Update the value and click **Save**

To edit several fields at once, use the full Edit form: click **Edit** at the top of the asset page, change the fields, and click **Save**.

See [Editing Asset Fields Inline](/knowledge-base/editing-asset-fields-inline) for the full list of fields that support inline editing and how it interacts with permissions and validation.
