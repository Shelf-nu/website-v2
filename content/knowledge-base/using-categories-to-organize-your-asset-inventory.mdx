## Setting Up Categories

Organizing your assets into custom categories helps ensure that everything is easily accessible and well-maintained. Shelf provides some default categories that you can use or remove as needed.


![Categories index](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/categories-1.webp)
### Creating an Asset Category

- Go to **Categories**
- Click **New category**
- Fill the **name** of your category. Any length works, so short names such as **IT** or **AV** are fine
- Fill a **description** (optional)
- Choose a **color** for your category: click the swatch left of the **Color** field to open your browser's color picker, type a hex code, or press the arrows button for a random color
- Check the **Preview** under the fields, then click **Create** to finish

![The new category form on the Categories page: Name AV, a green swatch next to the Color field holding #2E7D32, and under the row a Preview badge reading AV with the line "Badges use a light tint of your color with darker text, so the name stays readable. Your exact color is still saved."](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/category-color-picker.webp)

**Why the badge is lighter than the color you picked.** Category and tag badges draw a light tint of your color as the background, with darker text, so the name stays readable on any color. The **Preview** shows exactly how the badge will look, and the color you picked is the one Shelf saves.

**Typing a color.** `#2e7d32`, `2E7D32` and the short form `#fff` are all accepted; Shelf saves them as a full six-digit code such as `#ffffff`. Anything that is not a hex color is refused with *"Enter a hex color, like #4f46e5"*, shown under the row, and so is a blank name. Editing a category works the same way, with **Update** in place of **Create**.

Once created, you will be able to assign the newly created category to new and existing assets.

## Utilizing Categories

### Add or Adjust the Category of an Asset

1. Go to **Assets**, and choose an asset (or create a new asset)
2. Click **Edit**
3. Use the **Category** field to add or change the category of the asset
4. Click **Save** to save your changes

### Filter Your Asset Index by Category

- Go to **Assets**
- On the right hand side, click the **Categories** dropdown filter
- Select categories or find a category by typing
