## Getting Started

### What you need

- A **Team** workspace. Bookings are a Team feature, and bookings are what the calendar draws.
- A screen at least as wide as a tablet. On a phone-sized screen the toggle is hidden and the Assets index stays a list.

The view works in both index modes, the simple index and the [Advanced Asset Index](/knowledge-base/advanced-asset-index-complete-guide). You do not need to switch modes to use it.

### Opening the Availability View

1. Go to **Assets** in the sidebar.
2. Next to the search box there are two buttons: **List view** and **Availability view** (the calendar icon). Click the calendar icon.
3. To go back, click **List view**.

The choice is part of the page address (`view=availability`), so you can bookmark the calendar or send the link to a colleague.

## Understanding the Interface

### Calendar layout

- **Asset rows**: every asset on the current page of the index gets a row on the left, with its photo, status and category. The header shows how many that is, for example *20 assets out of 42*. Move to the next page of the index to see the rest.
- **Timeline**: dates run across the top. Switch between **Month**, **Week** and **Day**, step back and forward with the arrows, and jump back with **Today**.
- **Booking bars**: each bar is one booking holding that asset, drawn from its start to its end.

The calendar follows your own [date and time preferences](/knowledge-base/date-time-and-timezone-preferences): the first day of the week, 12 or 24-hour times, and your time zone.

![Availability view](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/knowledgebase/availability-view-2.webp)

### Which bookings appear

The calendar draws bookings that are **Reserved**, **Ongoing** or **Overdue**. Those are the bookings that hold an asset now or will hold it later. Drafts are not drawn, and neither are completed, cancelled or archived bookings.

Each bar is colored by the booking's status:

- **Blue**: Reserved
- **Purple**: Ongoing
- **Red**: Overdue
- **Green**: the asset has already come back from this live booking (see below)

### What a bar tells you

The bar itself shows the booking's name. If your role lets you see custody, it also shows the custodian.

Hover a bar for the details: the booking name and status badge, its start and end, the custodian, who created it, and its tags. If the asset is on the booking through a kit, the popup names the kit. For [quantity-tracked stock](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) it also shows how many units the booking holds.

Click a bar to open the booking in a new tab.

## When an Asset Comes Back Early

A booking often returns in pieces. Someone brings back one camera on Tuesday and keeps the rest until Friday. When an asset is checked in from a booking that is still **Ongoing** or **Overdue** (a [partial check-in](/knowledge-base/partial-check-ins-efficiently-handle-incomplete-returns)), its bar on that booking **stops at the moment it was checked in** and turns **green**.

- The bar reads **Returned** with the check-in time, followed by the booking name (and the custodian, if your role can see custody), so you can tell without hovering.
- The hover popup keeps the booking's real status and its full period, and adds a **Returned** line with the time. The booking itself is still live; only this asset has left it.
- The rest of the booking's assets keep their full purple or red bars until they come back too.

This matches how bookings already treat the asset. From the moment it is checked in, it is free: it shows up when you add assets to a new booking, and a new booking can reserve it.

A few cases keep the full bar:

- **Quantity-tracked stock** turns green only once **every** booked unit is back. A pool with some units still out keeps its full bar.
- If the asset goes out again on the same booking, its bar goes back to the booking's own color.
- An asset added to a booking after the booking went out, and never handed over, keeps the planned bar.
- The **Kits** index has its own availability view, and there a kit keeps its full bar until the booking ends.
- The **Bookings → Calendar** page draws one bar per booking rather than per asset, so it keeps showing the booking's full period.

## Using Filters and Search

The calendar shows the same assets as the list, so whatever you have set on the index applies: the search box, the filters, and the sort order of the rows. Filter to a category, a location or a custom field value, then switch to the calendar to see only that equipment.

- **Category**: filter to "Camera" to see only camera availability.
- **Location**: filter to "Studio B" to see what that location holds and when it is free.
- **Custom fields** (Advanced Asset Index): filter by a field such as "Project Type" to see project-specific equipment.

## Best Practices

### 1. Regular monitoring

- **Weekly reviews**: open the **Week** view every Monday for the coming week.
- **Monthly planning**: use the **Month** view for project planning and resource allocation.
- **Overdue follow-up**: red bars are bookings past their end date. Click one to open the booking and chase the return.

### 2. Strategic filtering

- **Project-based views**: filter by custom fields for project-specific planning.
- **Location-specific planning**: use location filters for multi-site organizations.
- **Equipment type focus**: use category filters for specialized equipment planning.

### 3. Team coordination

- **Share the link**: the calendar's address includes your filters, so a colleague who opens it sees the same equipment.
- **Booking coordination**: check the calendar before busy periods to spot assets that are already spoken for.
- **Early returns**: a green bar means that asset is back and can go out again before the booking ends.

### 4. Maintenance planning

- **Find the gaps**: look for stretches with no bars to schedule maintenance when nothing is booked.
- **Reminders live elsewhere**: the calendar draws bookings only. Track warranty expirations and service dates with [Asset Reminders](/knowledge-base/asset-reminders).

## Common Use Cases

### Event planning

**Scenario: planning a 3-day conference**

1. Filter the index to the "AV Equipment" category.
2. Open the **Week** view on the week of the event.
3. Find the projectors, cameras and audio equipment with no bar across the event dates.
4. Switch to **List view**, tick those assets, and click **Book selection** to start the booking.

### Production scheduling

**Scenario: a video production company scheduling shoots**

1. Filter by the "Camera Equipment" and "Lighting" categories.
2. Use the **Month** view to see equipment availability.
3. Plan shoots around the gaps.
4. Avoid conflicts with existing bookings.

### Maintenance coordination

**Scenario: an IT department planning maintenance windows**

1. Filter by the "IT Equipment" category.
2. Look for stretches with no bookings.
3. Schedule maintenance in those gaps.
4. Coordinate with any bookings that start soon after.

### Multi-location resource sharing

**Scenario: equipment sharing between office locations**

1. Filter by a specific location.
2. Identify equipment with few or no bookings.
3. Plan transfers between locations.
4. Coordinate with transportation schedules.
