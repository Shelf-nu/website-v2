A booking whose end time was less than 15 minutes away, or already past, completed in one click on **Check-in** or **Quick check-in**. Now every check-in asks first, in one **Check in booking** dialog: *"This checks in every asset of Studio Shoot and completes the booking."*, or *N assets* for a partial check-in, with **Cancel** and **Check in**. An early check-in keeps its question about moving the end date to now, as **Adjust Date** and **Don't Adjust Date** in the same dialog. See [Early Check-in and Check-out of Bookings](/knowledge-base/early-check-in-and-check-out-of-bookings#every-check-in-asks-first).

Also fixed on the asset form: after picking an [asset model](/glossary/asset-models), which adds the model's category to the page address, **Save** sent you back to the edit page with that category preselected even though you never saved it. Save now returns you to the page you came from, for example the filtered asset list you opened the asset from, and the saved category is the one the page showed.
