Swapping a part out of a kit while the kit is out on a job is ordinary maintenance. Until now it had an expensive side effect. Ending the booking worked out which kits to release by looking at what each asset belonged to **at that moment**, so a kit whose members had been pulled out during the job was invisible when the job ended, and it kept reading **Checked Out** with nothing out.

That is not a cosmetic badge. A kit stuck in that state cannot be added to a live booking, is filtered out of the booking picker and the add-to-kit picker, and there is no button anywhere in Shelf that clears it.

## What happens now

A booking keeps its own record of which kit each item went out under, and that record survives the member leaving the kit. Ending a booking now reads both — the kit's current contents and the booking's record — so a kit is found and released either way. This applies wherever a booking ends: **check-in**, **cancel** and **delete**, on a single booking or across a selection on the bookings index.

Two neighbouring cases are fixed by the same change:

- **A check-in spread over several sessions** now releases the kit once its last outstanding item is back. Earlier sessions no longer leave residue that keeps the kit reading **Checked Out** after everything has returned.
- **An asset that belongs to two kits** now releases both. Only one of them was ever cleared before.

Releasing a kit is deliberately generous rather than exact: a kit named once too often is a harmless repeat of "this is available", while a kit missed once is stuck for good.

Kits already stuck this way keep their status, because the booking that would have released them has already finished. [Contact support](/contact) with the kit name and we can correct the record.
