The advanced asset index now has an **Asset model view**, next to the list and availability views. It lists your [asset models](/glossary/asset-models) instead of individual assets: for each model, how many of the assets matching your filters carry it, where they are right now (in, out, in custody, plus how many are not bookable) and their total value. Your filters, saved filters and search all apply, so filtering to one location tells you which models you have there and how many. Assets without a model sit together in a **No model** row at the bottom, which is a handy place to start grouping a fleet.

Click a model's count to open a side panel with the assets behind it, in the columns you have set up for the list. Tick one or more models and use **Book selection** to create a booking that reserves units of them, or to add them to an open booking, with the number still free for that booking's dates shown beside each model.

The view counts individually tracked assets only, because asset models do not cover quantity-tracked stock.
