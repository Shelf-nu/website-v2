Until now Shelf only saved a scan when someone scanned an asset's **QR code**. Scanning a barcode or a SAM ID, scanning items into a booking, kit, location or custody list, and scanning in an audit left no trace, so for a team that labels with barcodes the **Last scan** card on the asset page never moved.

Every scan is recorded now, with what was scanned, where it was scanned from, and the asset or kit it resolved to. The **Last scan** card on an asset's **Overview** tab, and on a kit's page, shows the newest one and names the code in its **Source** row: *QR code*, *Barcode* with the value, or *SAM ID* with the ID. A scan that carried no location keeps the pin of the newest earlier scan that did, and says which scan that was. Scans from the web scanner and from the Shelf Companion app also leave a note on the asset's **Activity** tab that names the barcode or SAM ID. Scanning the same code twice within 30 seconds counts once.

Since 19 October the card also has a **View all scans** button that opens the asset's or kit's [full scan history](/knowledge-base/where-an-asset-was-last-scanned#the-full-scan-history), newest first.
