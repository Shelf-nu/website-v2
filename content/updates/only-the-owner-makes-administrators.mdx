Shelf now decides every role question in one place, and a few rules got stricter along the way. The ones you will notice:

- **Administrators come from the owner.** Only the workspace owner can invite someone as an Administrator or promote a member to Administrator, on every path: the invite dialog, a resent invite, the **Change role** dialog and a CSV user import. Administrators still invite and manage Self-service and Base users. A CSV file from an Administrator that contains an `ADMIN` row is refused as a whole.
- **Demotions hand records to an Administrator.** When you lower someone's role, the records they owned can go to the owner or another Administrator, not to a Self-service or Base member. An Administrator lowered by your identity provider's group mapping hands over the same way as one lowered by hand.
- **Self-service and Base change only their own drafts.** They can add items to their own booking, and delete it, only while it is a **Draft**. Self-service users can still take items off a **Reserved** booking and cancel it. Base users cannot cancel or archive a booking on any path.
- **Audit details belong to Administrators.** Editing an audit's details or changing who it is assigned to needs the Owner or an Administrator, and the assignee must be a member of the workspace.

The [roles and permissions guide](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions) has the full table.
