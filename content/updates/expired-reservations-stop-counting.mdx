A reservation whose end date passed without anyone checking it out reads gray **Expired** on the **Bookings** list, the booking page, an asset's or kit's **Bookings** tab and **My bookings**, and the **Status** filter has an **Expired** option that lists exactly them. It stops counting: the asset's status badge, its hover card and the **Reserved** figures leave it out, and the hover card says so on a line of its own: `N expired reservations (last ended <date>), not counted`. Nothing else about the booking changes, so it can still be archived, cancelled or checked out.

Checking one out asks first. **Check out** on the booking page opens **Reservation dates have passed** with two answers: **Check out with new end date** starts the booking now, ends it at the date you pick (proposed: now plus the planned length) and schedules the check-in reminder, after the same checks an extension passes; **Check out as overdue** keeps the dates and lands it in Overdue. A new end date becomes the booking's planned period, and the replaced one goes on its activity. The scanner, the fulfil scanner and **Check out selected items** offer **as overdue** only, and a check-out from the Shelf Companion phone app keeps the dates and lands the booking in Overdue.
