Shelf Companion now puts the news that is about you on your phone's lock screen. Update to **1.7.0** from the App Store or Google Play, open **Settings → Notifications** in the app and tap **Turn on notifications**.

You get a push when a booking you are the custodian of is reserved, changed, due to go out or come back, or overdue; when you are assigned an audit and before it is due; and when an asset reminder that includes you comes due. Tap it and the app opens that booking, audit or asset, switching workspace if it needs to. Switch off any kind, or all of them, in the app or under **Account settings → Notifications** on the web. Emails keep arriving as before.

Push is included in the **Plus**, **Team** and **Enterprise** plans.
