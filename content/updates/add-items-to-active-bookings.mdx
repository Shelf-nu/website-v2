Plans change after gear starts leaving the cage. Now you can add assets and kits to a booking that's already **Ongoing** or **Overdue**, right from the asset page, a kit page, the booking's own **Manage assets** modal, the bulk **Add to booking** panel, and both scanners.

The new items join the booking as line items and stay **Available** until you purposefully check them out. That matches how [progressive checkout](/knowledge-base/progressive-checkout-of-bookings) already works: the booking is "open" from the first item that leaves, and you keep handing gear out as it's picked up. Adding something mid-booking no longer forces it into a checked-out state you didn't ask for.

Shelf still protects you from double-booking: an item that's already checked out on another active booking can't be pulled onto this one. Everything else is fair game to add whenever you need it.
