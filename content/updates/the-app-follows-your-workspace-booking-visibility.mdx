A workspace can let Base and Self-service members see bookings they are not the custodian of, with the **Bookings visibility** toggle in **Settings ▸ Workspace ▸ Permissions**. The website has always honoured it. The [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app did not.

Those members got an empty **Bookings** tab, an empty calendar and empty booking sections on **Home**, in a workspace whose website listed every booking. A customer reported it from the field: a Base user reading two ongoing bookings in the browser and *"No active bookings"* on Android, same workspace, same moment.

Four screens now take their answer from the setting rather than from the role alone: the **Bookings** list, the calendar, a booking's detail screen, and **Home**. Opening a booking you are allowed to see answers instead of reporting it as missing.

Three things did not change, deliberately:

- **Drafts stay private to whoever created them**, toggle or no toggle, exactly as on the web.
- **Seeing a booking is not seeing who holds it.** The custodian's name stays behind the separate **Custody visibility** toggle, so a list can show you the booking and print **private** where the name would be. A booking with nobody assigned shows no custodian, which is a different thing.
- **Nothing about what you may do.** Reserve, check out, check in, cancel, archive, delete, and every add or remove of gear keep the ownership rules they already had.

**Nothing to install.** This is a server-side change, so every phone that already has Shelf Companion picked it up when it deployed, whatever version it is running. There is no app update to wait for.
