A pool of 14 sandbags is one record in Shelf, so one scan could only say "found". On the web audit scan page, scanning a [quantity-tracked asset](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) now opens **How many did you find?** with the expected units prefilled. Type what you counted, or click **All here**, and the row reads **8 of 14 pcs**; count more than expected and it reads **16 of 14 pcs (+2)**. Scan the pool again, or click **Edit count**, to correct it. The audit's statistics, the **Complete Audit** dialog and the receipt gain a **Units counted** line, and the receipt prints a **Quantity** column. A location audit expects the units placed at that location, a kit audit the units in the kit.

An audit photo uploaded from 9 November 2026 on carries two facts beside its thumbnail: where it came from (**Shelf app camera**, **Photo library** or **Web upload**) and when it was taken, read from the file. A photo taken more than a day before it was uploaded is flagged **Old photo**, and one with no capture time **No date**. A photo that is fine gets no chip. Photos uploaded before that date show their upload time and are not flagged, and a capture time is what the file says, so it can have been edited. The receipt prints the same under each photo, so a reviewer holding the paper can see which pictures the files date to the count.

Both work on the web, in any browser, including on a phone, and in the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app from version 1.8.0, which counts a pool in its **Count units** sheet and labels its photos **Shelf app camera** or **Photo library**. A scan of a pool from an older app version reads **Not counted** on the web, and its photos show as **Phone app**.
