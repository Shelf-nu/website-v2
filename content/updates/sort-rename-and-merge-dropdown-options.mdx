A dropdown custom field used to keep its options in the order they were typed, and the only way to fix a spelling was to delete the option and add it again, which left every asset holding the old spelling behind. Under **Settings → Custom fields**, an Option field's choices can now be managed in place:

- **Drag to reorder**, or press **Alt+Up** / **Alt+Down**, and **Sort A-Z** in one click, with **Undo sort**.
- **Rename** an option, and every asset holding it moves to the new spelling when you save.
- **Merge duplicates** that differ only in capitals or spaces (`Sony`, `sony `), keeping the first spelling.
- See **Used on N assets** on each option, and confirm before removing one that is in use.

Long dropdowns also get a search box above eight options, in the advanced index filters and in the Shelf Companion app.
