Editing an asset and clicking a sidebar link used to lose whatever you had typed, with no warning. Now the asset, kit and location forms (new and edit) and the form that edits an existing booking watch for changes.

As soon as a field differs from what is saved, an **Unsaved changes** bar appears, at the top of the page on a wide screen and along the bottom on a phone, with **Discard** and **Save**. **Discard** puts every field back to its saved value, including a category, tag or location you picked. **Save** saves as the form's own button does, and a refused save, for example for an empty name, keeps the bar. Leaving the page with unsaved work, by a link, the breadcrumb or the back button, asks *"You have changes that are not saved. Leave this page and lose them?"* with **Leave page** and **Stay**, and closing or reloading the tab asks too. Opening a dialog on the same page, such as **Manage assets** on a booking, is not leaving and asks nothing. See [Adding New Assets](/knowledge-base/adding-new-assets#unsaved-changes).
