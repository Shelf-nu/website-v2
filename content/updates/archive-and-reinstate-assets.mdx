Until now, the only way to get retired equipment out of your lists was to delete it, and a deleted asset takes its history with it. You can now **archive** an asset instead. Choose **Archive** from the asset's **Actions** menu, or archive a selection from the asset index. An archived asset leaves your asset lists, the booking picker, the dashboard and your current-inventory reports, but the record stays, with its history, SAM ID and QR code. **Reinstate** brings it back exactly as it was.

To find archived assets, click the list title on the **Assets** page and choose **Archived** or **All**; the same menu is on a kit's and a location's asset lists. An archived asset is read-only and carries an **Archived** badge everywhere it still appears, including bookings that already held it and the page its QR label opens. History reports such as Activity, Asset Utilization and Top Booked keep counting it.

Archiving is available to Owners and Administrators, on the web. For now it covers individually tracked assets that are not checked out, in custody or in a reserved or checked-out booking.
