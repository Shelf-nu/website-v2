Until now, the only way to get retired equipment out of your lists was to delete it, and a deleted asset takes its history with it. You can now **archive** an asset instead. Choose **Archive** from the asset's **Actions** menu, or archive a selection from the asset index. An archived asset leaves your asset lists, the booking picker, the dashboard's asset totals, value, status split and top locations, and the Asset Inventory, Asset Distribution and Idle Assets reports, but the record stays, with its history, SAM ID and QR code. **Reinstate** brings it back exactly as it was.

To find archived assets, click the list title on the **Assets** page and choose **Archived** or **All**; the same menu is on a kit's and a location's asset lists. An archived asset's details are read-only, though you can still add a note on its **Activity** tab, reinstate it or delete it. It carries an **Archived** badge everywhere it still appears, including bookings that already held it and the page its QR label opens. History reports such as Activity, Asset Utilization and Top Booked keep counting it.

Archiving is careful with everything around the asset. The asset's **Activity** tab records who archived and reinstated it, its reminders pause instead of emailing, and a kit whose assets are all archived can't be booked or put in custody until one comes back. A Draft booking that holds an archived asset names it and keeps **Reserve** greyed out until you take it off or reinstate it. Asset counts on the locations, kits, categories and asset models lists leave archived assets out.

In the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app, an archived asset you open or scan reads **Archived** rather than **Available**, its custody, move and edit actions are turned off, and Owners and Administrators can reinstate it from the phone.

Archiving itself is available to Owners and Administrators, on the web. For now it covers individually tracked assets that are not checked out, in custody or in a reserved or checked-out booking.
