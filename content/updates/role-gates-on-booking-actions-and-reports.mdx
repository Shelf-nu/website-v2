Two access rules that the app already intended are now enforced where it counts.

## The bulk Actions menu on a booking

On a booking's asset list, the bulk **Actions** menu asked only one question before opening: are you this booking's custodian. A Base user is the custodian of every booking they create, so past **Draft** the menu still offered them **Check in selected items** and **Remove assets/kits**. Check in came back as an error, which was merely annoying. Remove went through, and removing an item from a live booking sends that asset back to the available pool, so the two ended up doing the same thing by different routes. A customer reported it.

The per-row menu had the correct rule written into it all along. That rule now lives in one place and every surface asks it: the bulk menu, the action behind it, and the mobile remove endpoint.

| Role | Can take items off a booking while it is |
|---|---|
| Owner, Administrator | Draft, Reserved, Ongoing or Overdue |
| Self-service | Draft or Reserved, on their own booking |
| Base | Draft only, on their own booking |

A **Complete**, **Archived** or **Cancelled** booking is a closed record and its contents do not change for anyone. Nothing changes for Administrators and Owners, including the existing disabled-with-a-reason state on a closed booking. When a role holds none of the three bulk actions for the booking in front of it, the **Actions** button is no longer drawn at all rather than opening an empty sheet.

## The Reports area

**Reports** has been hidden from the sidebar for Base and Self-service users since it launched, but the pages themselves were gated on asset permissions as a stand-in, so a bookmarked or shared link still opened them. Reports read across every booking, custodian and asset value in the workspace at once, which is a wider question than either of the [visibility toggles](/knowledge-base/configure-what-self-service-and-base-users-can-see) answers.

The report pages, the CSV export and the PDF export now require the same access the sidebar has always implied: Administrators and Owners. Nothing changes for anyone reaching Reports through the app's own navigation, and reports remain included on every plan.
