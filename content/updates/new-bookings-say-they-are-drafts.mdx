Some bookings were still **Draft** when people came to pick up their gear: they had been created with their assets and never reserved, so nothing was held. The booking form now says what it makes. Above its buttons it reads *"This creates a draft. You can reserve it from the booking page."*, the button that creates a booking with its assets already chosen reads **Create draft**, and the confirmation is **Draft created**. On a draft, **Save** and the unsaved changes bar read **Save draft**.

When a draft's start time has passed, **Reserve** (or **Request reservation**) is greyed out with *"The start time has passed. Move it later to reserve."*, instead of a small error under the date. Move the start later and the button comes back before you save. Self-service and Base users also see why when the start is inside the workspace's minimum advance notice.

Checking a booking out in one click now asks first, the way check-in does. **Check out**, and **Check out remaining** on a booking that is already out, open a **Check out booking** dialog that names the booking; **Cancel** leaves it as it was. An early check-out still shows its date question instead, and the scanner and selection flows keep their own confirmation.
