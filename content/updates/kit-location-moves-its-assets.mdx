Kits are for gear that travels together, so a kit's location should tell you where its contents are. Until now it often didn't: changing a kit's location moved the kit record, but any member asset that already had a location of its own stayed exactly where it was. Since almost every asset has a location, the move quietly stopped at the kit.

A kit's location now owns its members' placement:

- **Changing a kit's location moves its assets** — from **Actions → Update location** on the kit page, from a multi-select on the **Kits** index, or by scanning the kit's QR code with the scanner set to **Update location**. Every asset that moves records an `ASSET_LOCATION_CHANGED` event and a note on its own timeline, so the history shows the move came from the kit.
- **Adding an asset to a kit that has a location moves the asset too**, instead of leaving the kit claiming a member that sits somewhere else.
- **Quantity-tracked stock moves only the kit's slice.** A kit holding 20 of 100 cables places 20 units at its location and leaves the other 80 wherever you put them. On the asset page that slice is labelled **via kit**.
- **Unpacking preserves placement.** Remove an individually tracked asset from a kit, or delete the kit, and the asset stays at the location the kit left it at rather than becoming unplaced. Clearing a kit's location does the same.

Nothing needs to be fixed up by hand. Existing kits whose members had drifted converge the next time that kit's location changes.
