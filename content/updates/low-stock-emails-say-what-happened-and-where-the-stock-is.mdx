A low-stock alert used to tell you that an item was low and little else. Now the subject reads *Low stock: AA batteries (2 Units left, minimum 5)*, or *Out of stock: AA batteries* at zero, and the body answers the questions you would have opened Shelf for: **What happened** (*2 Units used up by Dana Reyes on 24 Sep 2026 at 20:53*, with a link to the booking when one was involved), **Where it is** (the units at each location), **Out with people** (units in custody), and **Also low** (how many other items are at or below their minimum, linked to the list). A yellow box says to restock and adjust the quantity, and the footer says why you got the mail. The **Back in stock** email follows the same layout, with the recovered count in its subject.

Each owner and admin receives their own copy, greeted by name, with dates in their own format and time zone. Who gets the alert, when it fires and the once-per-crossing rule have not changed.

The **Low stock only** filter that the advanced asset index has had since August is now in the simple index too, as a button beside the search box, so the link in the email works whichever index you use. Details in [Low-stock alerts](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables#low-stock-alerts).
