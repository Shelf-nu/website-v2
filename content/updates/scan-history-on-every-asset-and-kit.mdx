Every scan of an asset or kit is recorded, whatever code was on the label and wherever the scan happened. The **Last scan** card shows the newest one, and under it a **View all scans** button that opens a panel with the asset's or kit's whole scan history, newest first, 25 at a time with **Load more**. Each scan says what was scanned (*QR code*, *Barcode* with its value, or *SAM ID* with the ID), where it was recorded (*QR link*, *Web scanner*, *Scanner list*, *Companion app*, *Audit* or *GPS update*; scans from before 16 October read *Not recorded*), when, who scanned it, the device, and the coordinates as a Google Maps link when the scan carried a location (a scan without one reads *No location*; web barcode and SAM ID scans carry none). The card's own **Source** row uses the same words.

The history is visible to the same people as the card: Owners, Administrators and Managers. Self-service and Base users do not see either.
