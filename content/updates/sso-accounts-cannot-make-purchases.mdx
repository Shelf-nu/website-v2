A university's payment card team asked whether students or staff could buy anything inside Shelf. Their people sign in with SSO, and until now an SSO member could still start a Team checkout from their Subscription page. Now an SSO user can start a purchase (a plan, a trial or an add-on) only for a workspace they own. Everyone else, Administrators included, sees *"Billing for single sign-on accounts is managed by your workspace owner."* instead of the plans. Existing subscribers can still manage or cancel in the billing portal, and accounts that sign in with a password see no change.
