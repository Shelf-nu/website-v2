When someone asks where an asset was kept and who moved it, Shelf now answers in two places.

**The Asset Activity Summary report** writes every change out in a **Details** column, with names: *Location changed: Gear Room → Studio B*, *Location set to Hull depot*, *Tags added: Fragile*, *Serial number changed: SN-1234 → SN-9999*. Quantity-tracked assets name the units that moved, in their own unit of measure, such as *12 units moved: Leeds unit → Hull depot*. Archiving an asset and reinstating it show up as **Archived** and **Reinstated** rows. The CSV carries each asset's **SAM ID** and **QR Code ID**, a **From** and a **To** column, and every row in the timeframe you picked, up to 100,000. That is a movement history you can hand to a client or an auditor as it is.

**A location's Activity tab** lists every asset that arrived or left, whether it was moved on the web, in the Shelf Companion app, together with its kit, or by changing where a quantity-tracked asset's units are kept. Each line names the location the asset came from or went to, when there was one, and **Export activity CSV** downloads the log.

Reports are part of the Plus and Team plans. See [Getting started with reports](/knowledge-base/getting-started-with-reports#read-the-asset-activity-summary) and [Exporting data from Shelf](/knowledge-base/export-exporting-workspace-data-from-shelf#exporting-other-data-types).
