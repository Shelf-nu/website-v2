Every booking check-out and check-in now records how it was made and where. The booking's activity line ends with it, for example *checked-out the booking (selected on the phone)* or *performed a partial check-in: Tripod (scanned on the web)*. A one-click action reads *in one click on the web* or *in one tap on the phone*. The [check-in receipt](/knowledge-base/print-a-check-in-receipt-for-a-booking) prints a **Check-in method** line when every item came back the same way, and the method under each row when they did not. Check-outs and check-ins from before 8 October 2026 carry no method.

The two settings under **Settings ▸ Workspace settings ▸ Bookings** that [remove the one-click check-out or check-in](/knowledge-base/require-explicit-check-in-and-check-out) for a role have new names and clearer words: **Check-in needs each item scanned or selected** and **Check-out needs each item scanned or selected**, with one switch for **Admins** and one for **Self Service users**. Each card now says, always visible, *"The workspace owner is never restricted. Base users cannot check out."* The switches and what they do are unchanged.

In the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app, where that rule hides **Check Out All Assets** or **Check In All**, the booking screen now says why: *"Your workspace requires each item to be scanned or selected to check out."*
