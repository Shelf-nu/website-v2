The advanced asset index now has a **Last audit date** column, on by default, so teams that run [audits](/features/audits) can see at a glance which assets have not been counted lately. It holds the completion date of the most recent completed or archived audit the asset was in, or *Never audited*. An asset reported missing in that audit still counts, because it was on the list that was checked. Hover a date for up to three recent audits with the asset's result in each, up to three upcoming ones, and **View all audits**, which opens the audits list filtered to that asset. Filter the column with the usual date operators or **Never audited**, sort it (never-audited assets go last), and take it along in a Standard export; the Import-ready export leaves it out.

The asset page shows the same history on a **Last audit** card in its right column: the last audit, up to three earlier and three upcoming ones, and **View all audits**.

Both appear for Owners, Administrators and Managers in a workspace with the Audits add-on. Without the add-on the column reads **Unlock audits** and there is no card; Self-service and Base users do not see the column or the card.
