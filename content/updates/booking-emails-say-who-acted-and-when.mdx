A booking email told you what happened to the booking, but not who did it or when. Now every booking email that a person triggers carries one line under the heading: **Reserved by**, **Completed by**, **Extended by**, **Reverted by**, **Cancelled by**, **Deleted by** or **Updated by**, followed by the person's display name and the moment, written in your own date format, time format and time zone. The plain-text version has the same line. Subjects are unchanged.

The completion email says **Completed by** rather than "Checked in by", since the person who completes a booking may not be the one who scanned every item back. Actions from the Shelf Companion app carry the line too. The scheduled reminder and overdue emails have no such line, because the scheduler sends them, not a person.
