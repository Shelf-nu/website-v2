An individually tracked asset that belonged to a [kit](/knowledge-base/kits) could be put into someone's custody on its own from the asset page, the bulk **Assign custody** action on the assets index and the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app, while the batch scanner already refused it. The kit then read **Available** while one of its parts was with someone.

Now every path refuses it with the same reason: *"Tripod" is part of kit "Interview Kit". Assign custody to the kit, or remove the asset from the kit first.* The menus and the phone's **Assign Custody** button are greyed out with that reason before you click. Assign custody to the kit, or take the asset out of the kit and then assign it.

Quantity-tracked stock is not affected: a kit holds only its slice of a pool, so the free units can still go to someone directly. Custody that was already assigned to a kit member stays where it is, and kit custody works as before. See [Custody a kit put there belongs to the kit](/knowledge-base/custody-feature-for-long-term-equipment-lend-outs#custody-a-kit-put-there-belongs-to-the-kit).
