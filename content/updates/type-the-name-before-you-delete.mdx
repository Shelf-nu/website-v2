One click on **Delete** used to remove an asset, or 200 of them, for good, and the dialog did not say what went with them. A customer asked us to make it harder to do by accident, so we did it for every delete in Shelf, on the web and in the Shelf Companion app.

Now every permanent delete tells you what it takes with it, and **Delete** stays greyed out until you type the item's name. For a bulk delete you type the number of items selected. Shelf checks what you typed on the server too, and if a **Select all** selection has grown since you opened the dialog, it deletes nothing and asks for the new number. This covers assets, kits, locations, bookings, asset models, custom fields, archived audits, and categories and tags that are in use.

We also fixed **Select all** on searched lists of locations, categories and asset models: deleting now removes exactly what the list shows, where before it could remove every one in the workspace.
