---
title: "Update an Asset's Location While It Is Checked Out"
description: "Update location in an asset's Actions menu is no longer greyed out while the asset is checked out on a booking. Record where the equipment actually is, and the move is logged like any other."
date: "2026-10-26"
layout: "update"
---

**Update location** in an asset's **Actions** menu used to be greyed out while the asset was checked out on a booking, with no reason given. You can now use it. The new location is saved and written to the asset's activity like any other move. For [quantity-tracked](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) assets, **Manage placements** is available the same way.

This matters when equipment goes out for a long time. Tools sent to a job site on a booking for weeks can now show the job site instead of the warehouse they left from. A checked-out kit could already be moved, taking its assets with it; single assets now work the same. The asset edit page, the bulk **Update location** action and the scanner already allowed it.

The booking still owns the asset's status and custody: the custody actions, **Archive** and **Delete** stay unavailable while it is checked out. An asset that sits in a kit still moves with its kit.
