The advanced asset list now shows each pool's stock right after Status.

- **Free now** reads like *14 of 20 pcs*: the units you could hand over today, after custody, kits and units out on bookings. Units promised to a future booking are still on the shelf, so they are not subtracted.
- **Stock status** gives one verdict per pool. **Short by N** means bookings, custody and kits need more than you own at the busiest point ahead. **None free**, **Running low** (at or below your min quantity) and **Enough** cover the rest. Without a min quantity it can still read **Short by N** or **None free**; otherwise it shows a dash. Hover for the figures, and for a shortfall, a link to the booking that causes it.
- **Reserved** (in the column picker) counts the units promised to upcoming bookings, and an over-committed pool reads like *12 · 2 short*.
- **Unit of measure** has its own column. **Total quantity** is now the bare number, and the Standard CSV export writes it that way too, so the column adds up in a spreadsheet.

Status on the list now uses the same figures, so a pool with units out says so instead of reading Available. All three new columns can be filtered and sorted, and **Set min quantity** in the bulk actions gives many pools a threshold in one step. The **Needs reordering** filter (formerly *Low stock only*) compares Free now with each pool's min quantity.

On the asset page and in the Shelf Companion app the same figure is now called **Free now** as well.
