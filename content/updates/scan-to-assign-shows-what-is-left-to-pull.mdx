When a booking reserves gear by [asset model](/glossary/asset-models), **Scan to assign** used to open a scanner that said nothing about the reservation: you scanned, and kept track of what was still missing yourself. It now shows the same progress as the check-out scanner. A summary line such as **2 models reserved**, with a **3 / 6** count and a progress bar, folds out into one bar per model. Underneath, every unit still to pull has its own **Pending** row, named after its model, and each matching scan moves the count and clears a row.

You can stop part way. Submitting a scan that covers only some of the reservation saves what you scanned and leaves the rest open, and the counts pick up from there next time. A unit that was already on the booking, without answering a reservation, can also be scanned here to count toward its model.
