You can now **subscribe** your everyday calendar to a live feed of your Shelf bookings, so reservations appear in the calendar you already use and stay up to date on their own. When a booking is added, moved, or cancelled in Shelf, your Google, Apple, or Outlook calendar reflects it on its next refresh — no re-exporting.

Open the **Calendar** page and click **Subscribe** (or go to **Account Settings → Calendars**) to get a secret feed URL. Add it to Google Calendar via **Other calendars → From URL**, or use the one-click `webcal://` link for Apple Calendar and Outlook. The feed is one-way and read-only, includes your confirmed bookings across a rolling window (about a month back to a year ahead), and every event links straight back to the booking in Shelf.

The feed mirrors what you can already see in Shelf: if you can view all workspace bookings, an admin can publish a **shared team calendar** everyone subscribes to; self-service and base members get a feed of just their own reservations. The URL is a secret, so treat it like a password — you can **regenerate** it to rotate the link or **stop sharing** to revoke it at any time. Free for all Team workspaces.

This read-only feed is the first step of deeper calendar integration; two-way calendar sync is on the roadmap.
