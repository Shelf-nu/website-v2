The person holding a piece of equipment is usually the first to know it is broken. Now they can tell you from the asset's own page. Every member of a Team workspace, Base and Self-service users included, has **Report a problem** in the asset page's **Actions** menu.

How it works:

- Open the asset, or scan its QR label with a phone camera to land on the same page.
- Choose **Actions**, then **Report a problem**, and describe what is wrong, for example that the battery does not charge.
- Press **Send**. The report is saved in the asset's history with the reporter's name, and emailed to the workspace's owners, administrators and managers.

The email names the asset and the reporter and quotes their words. Reply to it and your answer goes straight to the person who reported the problem.

Reports are plain text, sent from the web app, on assets in Team workspaces. The knowledge base article has the details.
