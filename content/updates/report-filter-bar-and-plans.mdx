Reports could always be filtered, but only by typing parameters into the address bar, so in practice nobody did. Every booking, asset and custody report now has a **filter bar** with dropdowns for the filters it supports: Category, Location, Status and Custodian where they apply, and two new ones on every asset-based report:

- **Custom field**: pick a field, then one of the values it holds, for example *Condition is Good*. Text, dropdown and yes/no fields; several combine so a row must match all of them.
- **Asset model**: narrow a report to the units of one or more models. **Asset Distribution** also gains a **By Asset Model** breakdown.

Applied filters show as chips you can remove one by one, and the headline numbers, the CSV and the PDF follow exactly what the page shows. **Monthly Booking Trends** now applies its category and location filters, which it used to accept and ignore. Filtered reports are still a link you can bookmark and share.

Since 20 October 2026, **Reports** is part of the **Plus and Team** plans. On a Free workspace it stays in the sidebar and shows which plans include it; the plan is the workspace's, so an Administrator in a Team workspace keeps reports whatever their own plan.
