On 8 October Shelf moved [every role decision into one place](/updates/only-the-owner-makes-administrators), and one rule came out stricter than intended: only the workspace owner could grant the Administrator role by invite, because invites inherited the owner-only flag that guards role changes. Before that, any Administrator could invite an Administrator.

That is back. An Administrator can invite someone as an **Administrator** from **Settings → Team → Users → Invite a user**, in a [CSV user import](/knowledge-base/inviting-users-via-csv-upload) with an `ADMIN` row, and by resending a pending Administrator invite. An import from an Administrator with an `ADMIN` row is no longer refused.

The owner keeps the actions that act on an existing member: promoting someone to Administrator with **Change role**, changing an Administrator's role, and revoking an Administrator's access. So an Administrator who invites a colleague as an Administrator cannot later demote or remove them; the owner can. The full table is in [User Roles and Their Permissions](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions#full-permissions-reference).
