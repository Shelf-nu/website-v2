Checking a list one record at a time used to mean opening a record, going back, and finding your place again. Since 28 October 2026 a record you open from its list has two arrows in the top right of its page. The up arrow opens the previous record of that list and the down arrow the next one, keeping the search, filters and sort you set, and stepping across page boundaries. The **[** and **]** keys do the same whenever you are not typing in a field or have a menu open.

It works on asset pages opened from **Assets** (simple and advanced mode), booking pages from **Bookings**, kit pages from **Kits** and location pages from **Locations**. On a booking the arrows sit in the title row, next to **Add to calendar**. A page opened from a link, a bookmark or a scan has no list behind it, so both arrows are greyed out with *"Open from a list to step through it"*.
