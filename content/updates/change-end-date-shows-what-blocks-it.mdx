**Change end date** on an **Ongoing** or **Overdue** booking moves its end earlier or later on the web. An earlier end frees the gear for other reservations after it, while the items stay checked out until they come back.

For a later end, the dialog shows how long the items booked by name stay free, and which booking needs them next: *"No other booking has these items reserved until 8 Nov 2026, 09:00. After that, Camera day needs Cam 1, Cam 2."* A **Use …** button fills in the booking's usual end time on the last day that still ends before that reservation starts. Reservations by model and the stock of quantity-tracked items are checked when you submit.

A submit past that point is refused with each booking in the way, the items it reserves and the day it starts. [Quantity-tracked](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) stock is counted as a pool over the added days, so it is refused only when the units really run short, with the numbers named. On the phone, the extend refusal lists the shared items after each booking name. And while the booking runs, every item row that another booking has reserved carries an amber **Needed** badge saying from when, with the booking named in its tooltip.
