The only date action on a running booking used to be **Extend booking**, and it refused any end earlier than the current one. A job that finished early kept its gear blocked for other reservations until someone checked it in.

On the web the action is now **Actions → Change end date**, on **Ongoing** and **Overdue** bookings, and it moves the end in either direction. Moving it earlier changes only the date: the items stay checked out until they are checked in (and the booking turns **Overdue** if they come back after the new end), while other bookings can reserve them for the time after it. Moving it later still checks the added days against other bookings. The booking's activity records who *extended* or *shortened* it, and the people on the booking get a **Booking extended** or **Booking shortened** email.

The [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app's **Extend booking** still only moves the end later. To shorten a booking, use the web.
