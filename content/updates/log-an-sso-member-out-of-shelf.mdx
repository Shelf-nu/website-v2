In a workspace that uses SSO, a person's role comes from your identity provider. When a change has to apply now, such as a demotion or someone leaving, an owner or Administrator can sign that person out of Shelf on every device, and the new role applies the moment they sign back in.

Owners and Administrators of an SSO workspace now have **Log out of Shelf** in a member's **Actions** menu, on **Team ▸ Users** and on the member's own page. After a confirmation, the member is signed out of Shelf on every device, including the Shelf Companion app, and gets the new role when they sign in again through your identity provider. It appears only for members who sign in through the workspace's SSO domain, and it is locked on yourself and on the workspace owner.
