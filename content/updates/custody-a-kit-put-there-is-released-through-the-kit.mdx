Give a Camera Kit to someone and every asset inside it goes into that person's custody with it. Each of those holds is recorded as coming from the kit. Until now, only the web app's kit screen paid that record any attention, and only by hiding a button. Nothing refused the release itself.

Releasing one member on its own deleted the hold anyway. The asset went back to reading **Available** while the kit page still said Alice had it and still listed the asset as a member. The two pages disagreed about the same piece of gear, and the availability figures took the asset's word for it. The web app's kit screen greyed the button out, but the asset page, the bulk **Release custody** action on the assets index and the Shelf Companion app all went straight through. The app in particular drew a live green **Release Custody** button on any kit member.

## What happens now

Releasing a kit-derived hold is refused, under **Custody is managed by the kit**, with *"This asset is in custody because its kit is. Release the kit's custody instead."* Handing that member to a different person is refused for the same reason. Both answers are the same wherever you are: the asset page, the bulk action, and the app.

There are two ways to end the hold, and both go through the kit:

- **Release the kit's custody**, which clears every member hold it created.
- **Take the asset out of the kit**, which takes the kit's hold with it. The asset returns to **Available** unless something else is still holding it.

Custody you assigned to an asset yourself is untouched and releases exactly as before, including on an asset that sits in a kit and carries both kinds of hold at once. A bulk **Release custody** over a mixed selection is refused whole rather than half applied, so a batch never leaves you guessing which assets went through.

Quantity-tracked pools are unaffected. A pool can be held by several custodians and by a kit at the same time, so **Assign** and **Release Quantity Custody** act on one custodian's slice by name, and releasing yours never touches the units the kit holds.
