The role pickers in **Invite team members** and **Change role** used to list only the names: Administrator, Manager, Self service and Base. With Manager new, that left people guessing whether it was another word for Administrator. Each role now carries one plain sentence under its name. Manager, for example, reads *"Runs bookings, custody and audits for everyone, but cannot create, edit or delete assets, or change workspace settings or registered team members."* A **Learn more about roles** link under both pickers opens [User Roles and Their Permissions](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions).
