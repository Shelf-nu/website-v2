A spreadsheet runs a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` as a formula when you open the file, so a value someone typed into Shelf could act as a formula on your computer. Shelf's CSV exports now put a single quote (`'`) in front of such a value, and Excel and Google Sheets show it as plain text. Negative numbers such as `-5` or `-$5.00` keep working as numbers, so totals still add up.

This covers both asset export formats, Standard and Import-ready, and bookings, report exports, non-registered members and activity notes. Upload an asset export to update or import your assets and Shelf removes the added quote again, so nothing changes in your data. See [Exporting Data from Shelf](/knowledge-base/export-exporting-workspace-data-from-shelf#values-that-look-like-formulas) for the details.
