The item list on a booking now sorts the way you actually work through it.

- **Sort by Item type** groups kits first, then standalone assets, so you can process a bundle in one pass.
- **Sort by Status** now sinks fully checked-out items to the bottom, keeping the things you still need to hand out on top. That's especially handy during [progressive checkout](/knowledge-base/progressive-checkout-of-bookings), where a booking can hold a mix of Available and Checked out items at once.
- When you sort by any other field, kits and standalone assets are **interleaved** by that field instead of always pinning kits to the top, while each kit's members stay grouped together.

A small tooltip next to the sort control explains what **Status** and **Item type** do, so you never have to guess which one to pick.
