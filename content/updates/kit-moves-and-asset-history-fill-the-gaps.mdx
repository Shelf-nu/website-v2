**Kits.** An individually tracked asset can only be in one kit, so adding one that already sits in another kit moves it and takes it off that kit's draft and reserved bookings. The add dialog now says so first with a **Kit move notice** naming each asset and the kit it leaves, for example *"Camera A (from Box 1)"*. The same dialog no longer claims that assets added to a kit with no location lose their location: they keep it, and always did. And **Assign Custody** on a kit whose member is already in someone's custody now names that member, *"Some assets in the kit are already in custody: Canon R5. Release their custody first."*, instead of failing with a generic error.

**Asset history.** A move between kits reads *changed kit from Box A to Box B*, where the kit it left used to be blank, and deleting a kit now leaves a line on every member. Turning **Available for bookings** on or off, one asset at a time, in bulk or by CSV update, is recorded with who did it, and shows in the Asset Activity Summary report. Whole-booking check-outs and check-ins write a line on each asset whichever way they were made, scanned in full or from the phone included, where before only the web's one-click buttons did.
