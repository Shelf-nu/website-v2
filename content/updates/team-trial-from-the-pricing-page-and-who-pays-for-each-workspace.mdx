Two changes to how plans show up in Shelf, one for new accounts and one for everyone.

## The Team trial you picked is the Team trial you get

Clicking **Start Free Trial** on the Team plan on the [pricing page](/pricing) used to take you to a plain signup form. Once your account was ready, Shelf asked again whether you wanted a Personal or a Team workspace, as if you had never chosen.

Now the signup page confirms the choice in one line under the heading: *"You're starting a Team trial. 7 days, no credit card."* After you enter the code from your email and finish onboarding, Shelf skips the second question and opens the plan page with the Team trial first. If you change your mind, the link under the plans, **Continue with a free Personal workspace instead**, does the same as choosing Personal.

A signup without a plan in the link works exactly as before. People who join through a workspace invitation still land in that workspace.

## Every workspace's plan, in one table

The **Subscriptions** page (open it from the menu under your name) now starts with **Your workspaces' plans**. It has one row for each workspace you belong to, with its **Plan**, **Your role**, and **Paid by**.

A workspace's plan comes from its owner's subscription. So an Administrator in a paid Team workspace used to open this page and read that they were on the free version. The table now shows the Team plan and the owner who pays for it. For other people's workspaces, it shows only the plan name and the owner's name. Their prices, invoices, and add-ons stay private. A free workspace reads **No one** under **Paid by**.

Below the table, **Billed to you** lists only what you pay for yourself. If you work in someone else's paid workspace and pay for nothing, it offers two options: **Start your own Team workspace** and **Upgrade your personal workspace**. If you sign in through SSO, you have no personal workspace in Shelf, so only the first option appears.
