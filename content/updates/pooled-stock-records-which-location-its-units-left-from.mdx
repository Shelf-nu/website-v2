A [quantity-tracked asset](/knowledge-base/quantity-tracked-assets-and-consumables) can be kept at several locations at once: 60 cables in the stockroom, 40 in the van. Until now, nothing recorded which of those locations a unit came from when it went into someone's custody or out on a booking. When units were used up, Shelf could not tell which location had lost them, so the placements drifted above the total, and a later loss was refused with *"Lower the placements first."*

Now, for a pool kept at **two or more locations**, Shelf asks once where the units come from and remembers the answer:

- **Assign custody** gains a **From location** field, pre-filled with the location that has the most units left. The quantity is capped at what that location has, and asking for more is refused with the numbers: *"Debriefing Room has only 2 pcs."* The web scanner's **Assign custody** action asks on each such row too.
- **Check out** on a booking asks the same question, in a short confirmation on the one-click button or on the pool's row in **Check out selected items** and **Scan to check out**. The booking row then reads *from Studio* beside its status.
- **Adjust** gains an **At location** field: a restock lands there, a loss comes off there.
- The custody list shows the location after each person's quantity, the placements card shows **N in custody** under a location, and a location's **Assets** tab reads *· 40 pcs at this location · 10 on a booking*.
- **Release**, **Mark as consumed** and check-in take used-up units off the location they came from, so the counts stay true without a correction afterwards. Returned units change no placement.

A pool kept at one location, with or without unplaced units, and every individually tracked asset look and behave exactly as before. Holds recorded before this read *location not recorded* on the custody list.

The [Shelf Companion app](/mobile-app) does not ask yet. A check-out from the phone records the location with the most units left, and a custody assignment from the phone is recorded without a location.
