Exporting assets and importing them somewhere else used to mean reshaping the file by hand: the export was built for people to read, and the importer wanted its own column names and value formats. **Export selection** on the Assets index now asks which you want.

Pick **Import-ready** and Shelf writes the file with the importer's own headers and values, including quantity-tracking fields, asset models, barcodes, and custom fields with their types. Upload it into another workspace via **Assets → Import** and the importer creates the categories, tags, locations, custodians, models, kits, and custom fields it finds by name. Two things stay behind: QR IDs, which belong to the workspace that issued them, and asset images, which need re-adding after the import.

The same dialog also lets you choose whether the export covers only the **visible columns** in your index or **all columns**, on both formats. **Standard** exports are unchanged otherwise, and your current search and filters still apply when you use **Select all entries**.
