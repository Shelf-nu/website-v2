---
title: "Duplicating a Booking Keeps Its Model Reservations"
description: "Duplicate now copies a booking's model reservations along with its named assets, reserving each model up to what is free for the new dates and naming any that came up short."
date: "2026-10-30"
image: "/images/updates/add-assets-to-bookings.jpg"
layout: "update"
---

**Duplicate** on a booking used to copy only the assets picked by name, so a booking planned by model came back with none of its reservations. The copy now keeps them. It reserves what the source still had open: units already scanned in come across as named assets, so a reservation of 10 with 6 scanned becomes 6 named assets plus a reservation of 4.

Each model is reserved up to what is free for the dates you pick, instead of the copy being refused. When a model comes up short, the confirmation names it, for example *"Only 1 of 4 × Camera could be reserved for the new dates."* The details are in [Adding Assets and Kits to a Booking](/knowledge-base/adding-assets-and-kits-to-a-booking#duplicating-a-booking-keeps-its-model-reservations).
