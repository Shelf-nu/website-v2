The [Manager](/knowledge-base/user-roles-and-their-permissions#manager) role runs everyone's bookings, custody and audits, and until now it could only read the catalogue. If the person at the counter noticed a camera filed under the wrong location, they had to find an Administrator to fix it.

The workspace owner can now change that with one switch. Under **Workspace settings → General → Permissions**, the **Managers** section has **Add and edit assets and kits**. Switched on, Managers can create and edit assets and kits, update locations one at a time or for a selection, and link or swap QR codes and barcodes, on the web and in the [Shelf Companion](/mobile-app) app. They still cannot delete or archive anything, run CSV imports, or create categories, locations, tags, custom fields or asset models. The switch is off in every workspace until the owner turns it on, and only the owner can change it.

The Permissions card now also opens each role's section (Managers, Self service and Base) with one line saying what that role does by default, so you can see the starting point before you change anything.
