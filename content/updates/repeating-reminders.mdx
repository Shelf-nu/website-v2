Until now a reminder fired once. A yearly calibration, a quarterly safety check or a monthly stock count meant setting the next reminder by hand after each one. Since 27 October 2026 a reminder can **repeat**.

Open the asset, choose **Actions → Set Reminder**, and next to the date you will find **Repeat**: **Never** (a one-time reminder, as before), **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Every 2 weeks**, **Monthly**, **Every 3 months**, **Every 6 months** or **Yearly**. Add an **Ends on** date if the series should stop, or leave it open until you delete it. Editing an existing reminder offers the same field.

What a repeating reminder does:

- **Goes out at the same time each occurrence**, in the time zone of the person who set it. Weekly cadences keep the weekday; monthly and longer ones keep the day of the month, and a series set on the 29th to 31st moves to the last day of a shorter month and stays on that day afterwards.
- **Says what comes next.** Each email names the next date, and the last email of a series with an end date says it was the last one. Every occurrence leaves a note on the asset, so the asset's activity is the service log.
- **Shows its cadence** under the date on the **Reminders** page, the asset's reminders and the **Upcoming reminders** card on Home, and always lists the next occurrence due.
- **Stays editable** for as long as it runs. Editing changes the whole series; set Repeat to **Never** to turn it back into a one-time reminder, or delete it to stop it.
- **Pauses with an archived asset.** While the asset is archived the reminder shows **Paused** and sends nothing; reinstate the asset and it continues from the next date.

Repeating reminders are part of the **Plus and Team** plans. On the free Personal plan the Repeat field stays on **Never**, and one-time reminders work as they always have. A workspace that moves to the free plan with repeating reminders already set gets one more email saying that later ones will not be sent; the series resumes when the plan includes it again.

Reminders are still set by hand: they are not driven by a date custom field such as *Next calibration*.
