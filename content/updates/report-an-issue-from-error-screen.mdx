---
title: "Report a Problem Straight From the Error Screen"
description: "If something goes wrong, a Report this issue button now opens the feedback form prefilled with the error details, and screenshots you attach come through in full resolution."
date: "2026-07-06"
layout: "update"
---

When Shelf hits an unexpected error, the error screen now shows a **Report this issue** button. Clicking it opens the feedback form already filled in with what went wrong, so you can send it to our team in a couple of clicks instead of trying to describe it from memory.

We also fixed the screenshots you attach to any feedback. They used to arrive as tiny, unreadable thumbnails; now they come through at full resolution (bounded to a sensible size), so the support team can actually see what you saw. Each report also carries the context we need to reproduce a problem quickly, such as the page you were on and your app version.
