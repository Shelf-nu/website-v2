Shelf's lists are full of things that name a set of records: a category chip, a tag, a custodian's name, the number of assets in a location. Until now they were plain text, so getting from "12 assets in Studio B" to those twelve assets meant opening the asset index and building the filter yourself. Since 26 October 2026 each of them is a link to the list it describes, already filtered.

**Chips.** On any asset list (the index, a location's, kit's or booking's assets, an audit, the newest assets on **Home**), a **category** chip opens the asset index filtered to that category, and an **Uncategorized** chip opens the assets without one. A **tag** chip does the same for its tag, including the tags tucked behind a **+N**. A **custodian** chip on an asset, kit, location or audit list opens that person's assets; on the **Bookings** list it opens their bookings. Someone added by name without a Shelf account, or a viewer whose role cannot open team pages, gets the asset or bookings index filtered to that person instead.

**Counts.** On **Categories**, the asset count opens the index filtered to the category. On **Locations**, the **Child locations**, **Assets** and **Kits** counts open the location's page and its two tabs. On **Tags**, two new columns, **Assets** and **Bookings**, count what carries each tag and open those lists. On **Audits**, the **Expected**, **Found**, **Not scanned** (called **Missing** on the overview once the audit is completed) and **Unexpected** counts open the audit's overview with that filter set. On **Home**, the **Assets by status** legend opens the asset index filtered to **Available**, **In custody** or **Checked out**. In advanced mode, the asset count under **Workspace settings → Asset models** opens the index filtered to that model.

A few things are deliberately unchanged. On **Locations**, **Tags** and **Audits** a zero stays plain text, since there is nothing to open. Pickers keep working as before, because a click on a picker row toggles a selection. The counts match what the linked list shows by default, so archived assets, and archived or cancelled bookings, are left out. A link only appears when your role may open its target; a custodian chip in particular follows the same custody-visibility rule that decides whether you see the name at all. Everything works in both the simple and the advanced asset index, and a filtered link copied from the simple index (a tag or custodian filter in the address) now opens filtered for a colleague on the advanced index too.
