A booking used to print one fixed PDF, so the pick list for the warehouse, the sheet a client signs and the priced list for the office all had to be the same page. Now a workspace keeps named **PDF documents**, and **Actions → Generate PDF** asks which one to print. The PDF is titled with the document and the booking, and Shelf offers the document you picked last first next time.

Every workspace on a paid plan starts with two. The **Checklist** is the PDF you know. The **Check-out receipt** is the signed list of what went out: items A to Z, no descriptions or values, and signature lines for the custodian and the person issuing the gear. Owners and Administrators can add more under **Settings → General → PDF documents**: switch the image, quantity, kit, category and location columns on or off, print up to three custom fields as columns, group by category, sort by name, and choose whether the booking description, total value and signature lines print. One document is the default; any can be duplicated as a starting point.

On a workspace whose plan does not include PDF documents nothing changes: every booking prints the Checklist, and the settings card shows an upgrade note. The menu item that used to read **Generate overview PDF** is now **Generate PDF**.
