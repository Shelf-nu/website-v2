A team workspace keeps named **PDF templates** for its bookings, so the pick list for the warehouse, the sheet a client signs and the priced list for the office each print in their own layout, with custom fields as columns and a signed **Check-out receipt** among them. **Actions → Generate PDF** asks which one to print. The PDF is titled with the template and the booking, and Shelf offers the template you picked last first next time.

Every team workspace starts with two. The **Checklist** is the PDF you know. The **Check-out receipt** is the signed list of what went out: items A to Z, no descriptions or values, and signature lines for the custodian and the person issuing the gear. Owners and Administrators can add more under **Settings → Bookings → PDF templates**: switch the image, quantity, kit, category and location columns on or off, print up to three custom fields as columns, group by category, sort by name, and choose whether the booking description, total value and signature lines print. One template is the default; any can be duplicated as a starting point.

A personal workspace prints the Checklist on every booking and has no template picker. The menu item that used to read **Generate overview PDF** is now **Generate PDF**.

*Updated 10 November 2026: templates were introduced on 30 October as "PDF documents" under Settings → General and were renamed and moved to Settings → Bookings on 9 November.*
