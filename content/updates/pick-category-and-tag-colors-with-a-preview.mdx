Choosing a color for a category or tag no longer means typing a hex code. Click the swatch next to the **Color** field and your browser's color picker opens; the code fills in for you. A **Preview** under the fields shows the badge as it will look, with one line explaining why it is lighter than the color you picked: badges use a light tint with darker text so the name stays readable, and your exact color is still saved.

The forms are also easier to save. Short names such as **IT** or **AV** are accepted (they used to need three characters), colors typed as `#fff` or without the `#` are fine, and when something is wrong the message now appears under the fields instead of the **Create** or **Update** button doing nothing. See [Using Categories](/knowledge-base/using-categories-to-organize-your-asset-inventory#creating-an-asset-category).
