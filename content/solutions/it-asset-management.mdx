# IT Asset Tracking

**IT asset tracking** is keeping a record of every device your organization owns (laptops, Chromebooks, tablets, monitors, phones and their accessories) and of who has each one, where it is, and when it is due back, from the day it is bought to the day it is retired.

**Shelf is built for the physical side of IT:** devices that are handed to people, lent out and returned. Each device gets a QR code; IT staff and users scan it to check it out, return it, or confirm it during an audit.

**Shelf is not:**
- **Network discovery.** It does not install agents or scan your network to find devices. You add devices by CSV import or by hand.
- **Software license management.** It tracks physical things, not seats and subscriptions.
- **Device management (MDM).** It cannot lock, wipe or locate a lost laptop. Pair it with your MDM tool for that.

## What Is IT Asset Tracking?

IT asset tracking covers the full lifecycle of devices: procurement, deployment, assignment, usage, maintenance, and eventual retirement or replacement. It provides visibility into who has what, where devices are located, when they're due for return, and whether they need service.

Modern IT asset tracking goes beyond static inventory lists. It supports dynamic workflows like loaner programs, hybrid-workforce device assignments, onboarding/offboarding handoffs, and multi-location visibility—all from a centralized system.

---

## Why Teams Need a Modern IT Asset Tracking Solution

IT departments face persistent challenges with device management:

- **Missing devices**: Laptops, chargers, and peripherals disappear without clear trails.
- **No ownership history**: When devices change hands, responsibility becomes unclear.
- **Outdated spreadsheets**: Manual records drift out of sync and fail under real operational pressure.
- **Hybrid work complexity**: Remote employees, rotating offices, and distributed teams make visibility harder.
- **Disconnected systems**: Reservations, custody, and maintenance live in separate tools—or don't exist at all.

A modern IT asset tracking solution consolidates these workflows into a single, intuitive platform that fits how IT teams actually work.

### Quantity Tracking for Stockroom Accessories

Serialized devices — laptops, monitors, tablets — stay individually tracked, each with its own QR code and custody history. The interchangeable accessories you keep in bulk don't need that. Chargers, dongles, USB-C adapters, and spare cables can be tracked by quantity instead: one record per type, a running count, and a unit of measure. Set a minimum threshold on each and the workspace owner and every admin get a low-stock email before the dongle drawer runs dry — so reordering happens before a new hire is left waiting.

## IT Workflows in Shelf

### Onboarding and offboarding

Select a laptop, charger and headset and assign them to a new hire in one step, as custody. When someone leaves, filter the asset list by that person as custodian to see exactly what needs to come back, and release each item as it is returned.

### Loaner pools

Keep a pool of loaner laptops that staff or students book for set dates. Shelf blocks overlapping bookings, shows what is available now, and lists anything overdue.

### Chromebook and tablet carts

Group a cart's devices into a [kit](/features/kits) so the cart goes out and comes back as one unit, and Shelf shows which device is missing at check-in. A [scan audit](/features/audits) confirms every device is where it should be.

### Serial numbers and warranties

Every asset gets a sequential SAM ID. Add the serial number as a text field and the warranty end as a [date field](/knowledge-base/custom-field-types-in-shelf), then set a [reminder](/knowledge-base/asset-reminders) for the date so expiring warranties come up before they lapse.

### Single sign-on

Staff can sign in with Microsoft Entra, Google Workspace, Okta or another SAML 2.0 provider through the SSO add-on on the Team plan. See [pricing](/pricing).

### Moving from a spreadsheet or Snipe-IT

[Import your device list from a CSV](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide); categories, locations and custom fields are created as they are imported. Existing barcode labels can stay on the devices with the [Alternative Barcodes](/knowledge-base/alternative-barcodes) add-on. Comparing tools? See [Shelf vs Snipe-IT](/alternatives/snipe-it).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is IT asset tracking?

IT asset tracking is keeping a record of the devices an organization owns and of who has each one, where it is and when it is due back. Most teams label devices with QR codes or barcodes and scan them when they change hands, go out on loan, or are counted in an audit.

### What is the difference between IT asset tracking and IT asset management?

IT asset tracking follows physical devices: who has them, where they are, and whether they came back. IT asset management (ITAM) is broader and also covers purchasing, contracts, software licenses and disposal, often with network discovery agents. Shelf does the tracking side and leaves licenses and discovery to dedicated ITAM tools.

### How does Shelf handle device assignments?

Shelf uses custody to record who has each device. Assign it when you hand the device over, or let users check it out on a booking by scanning its QR code; returning it releases the custody and keeps the history.

### Can I track accessories like chargers and cables?

Yes. Use kits to group accessories with their parent devices, or [track consumables by quantity](/features/consumables-tracking) for the interchangeable ones you keep in bulk — one record per type with a running count and low-stock alerts. Shelf flags missing kit components when items are checked in or out.

### Does Shelf work for hybrid or remote teams?

Yes. Devices can be assigned to remote employees, and location updates can reflect home offices, shipping, or any custom location.

### How do I prevent double-bookings for loaner devices?

Shelf's booking system shows real-time availability and prevents conflicts automatically. Users see open time slots and reserve devices without overlap.

### How much does Shelf cost for an IT team?

Shelf is free for one user with unlimited assets. The Team plan, which adds bookings and unlimited users, is $67 a month or $370 a year; SSO and Audits are add-ons. See [pricing](/pricing).

### Is Shelf suitable for multi-site IT operations?

Yes. Use workspaces to separate inventories by site, department, or team—while maintaining centralized visibility for IT leadership.
