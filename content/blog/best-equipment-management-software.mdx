**Equipment management software** is a system that keeps a record of every piece of equipment an organization owns and tracks who has it, where it is, when it is booked and when it is due for a check. It replaces sign-out sheets and spreadsheets, so shared gear stops getting lost, double-booked or skipped for service.

**What to look for in equipment management software:**
- **Scanning anyone can do:** QR codes or barcodes read with a phone camera. RFID and GPS tags help in some settings, but cost more per item.
- **Check-out and custody:** a record of who has each item, since when, and when it is due back.
- **Bookings:** reservations for future dates that block double-bookings, if many people share the same gear.
- **Kits:** items that travel together go out and come back as one unit.
- **Maintenance:** reminders for inspections and service; work orders if you run a full maintenance program.
- **Audits and reports:** scan-based stock checks, utilization, and overdue items.
- **A pricing model that fits your team:** per user, per admin, per item or flat per workspace makes a large difference as you grow.

There are dozens of equipment management tools on the market. Most "best of" lists are written by one of the vendors, who — surprise — rank themselves first on every axis.

This page is different. We built Shelf, so we have a perspective. But we also have real competitors who do specific things better than we do, and we'll say so. Our goal is to help you find the right tool for your situation — even if that's not us.

## Three Categories, Not One

Before comparing tools, it helps to understand that "[equipment management software](/solutions/equipment-management)" means different things to different people. The market breaks into three categories:

**1. Equipment checkout and booking tools** — built for tracking who has what, scheduling shared gear, and preventing double-bookings. Best for schools, media production, shared equipment pools.
*Tools: Shelf, Cheqroom*

**2. Asset inventory and registry tools** — built for cataloging what you own, where it is, and its lifecycle stage. Best for IT departments, fixed asset tracking, compliance.
*Tools: Snipe-IT, Reftab, Sortly*

**3. Maintenance and operations platforms (CMMS)** — built for scheduling maintenance, tracking work orders, and managing equipment health. Best for facilities, manufacturing, fleet management.
*Tools: EZOfficeInventory (bridges categories 2 and 3), Asset Panda (bridges categories 1 and 2)*

If you need equipment booking and checkout, comparing Shelf to a CMMS tool is apples to oranges. If full preventive maintenance with work orders is your primary need, you need a tool in category 3. Knowing your category narrows the field immediately.

## The Tools

### Shelf

**What it is:** An open-source equipment management platform built around QR code scanning, equipment booking, and custody tracking. Shelf is what we make — so take this section with that context.

**Strengths:**
- **Flat pricing with unlimited users** — $67/month for the Team plan covers your entire organization. No per-seat fees. This matters most for schools and large teams where dozens of people need checkout access.
- **QR-first workflow** — scanning is the primary interaction, not data entry. Students, staff, and field workers pick it up without training.
- **Equipment booking** — visual calendar with conflict prevention. Reserve shared gear ahead of time (Team plan).
- **Kit management** — bundle a camera with its lens, battery, and SD card, on every plan. When you scan a booking back in, any kit item not yet returned stays listed as pending.
- **Open source** — full codebase is public. Self-host if you need to. No vendor lock-in.
- **Free plan** — unlimited assets for one user. Genuinely usable, not a demo.
- **Native iOS + Android app + PWA** — the free Shelf Companion app is on the App Store and Google Play (field workflows: scan, audit, custody, bookings). The web app also runs as a PWA in any modern phone browser.

**Limitations — where we'll be honest:**
- **Maintenance reminders, not full CMMS** — Shelf has asset reminders (one-time on every plan, repeating on Plus and Team), and each reminder it sends is noted in the asset's activity. You record the service itself in notes and custom fields. There is no dedicated service log, no work orders, no parts inventory and no technician dispatching. If full CMMS is your primary need, look at EZO, Asset Panda or a dedicated maintenance platform.
- **No depreciation or fixed asset accounting** — Shelf tracks operational use, not financial lifecycle. Snipe-IT or Asset Panda are better if depreciation reporting is required.
- **The mobile app is a field client, not a second admin console** — the Shelf Companion app covers scanning, audits (including notes and photos on individual scans), custody handoffs, and bookings. Setting audits up, bulk imports, custom field configuration, user and role management, reporting, and integrations stay on the web app.
- **No help desk integrations** — no Zendesk, Jira, or ServiceNow connectors. EZO (Zendesk, Jira) and Asset Panda (Zendesk, Jira, ServiceNow) have these.
- **Smaller ecosystem** — Shelf has fewer integrations and a smaller community than tools that have been on the market for 10+ years.

**Best for:** Schools and universities (unlimited users, no per-seat cost), media and production companies (booking + kits), operations teams managing shared equipment, anyone who values open source and pricing transparency.

**Pricing:** Free (1 user, unlimited assets) → Plus $34/mo (1 user, adds reports and repeating reminders) → Team $67/mo (unlimited users, adds bookings) → Enterprise (custom). [See pricing →](/pricing)

### Cheqroom

**What it is:** An equipment management platform focused on AV, media, and production environments. Cheqroom specializes in equipment checkout, booking, and kit tracking for teams that share professional gear.

**Strengths:**
- **Purpose-built for media/AV** — their workflows are specifically designed for camera cages, production equipment rooms, and studio gear management.
- **Booking and reservations** — calendar-based scheduling with availability views.
- **Kit management** — unlimited kits on every plan.
- **Unlimited users and items on every plan** — only admins pay (plus operators, if you add work orders). SSO, Slack, API, webhooks and Zapier are on every plan.
- **Native mobile apps on iOS and Android** — both platforms today.
- **Higher-ed experience** — a dedicated higher-education page and published university customer stories.

**Limitations:**
- **Expensive per-admin pricing** — $184 per admin per month on Core, $275 on Business and $367 on Enterprise, billed annually. Every plan starts at 3 admins, so the smallest Core contract is $552 a month. Core is limited to 1 location and leaves out reporting, data export, custom roles, PDF documents, audits and depreciation; those start on Business.
- **No free plan** — there is a 7-day free trial in a workspace pre-filled with sample items, kits and reservations (no credit card), but nothing free to keep using after that.
- **Closed source** — no self-hosting option, no code transparency.
- **Reporting starts on Business** — Core has no standard reporting, and enterprise reporting is on the Enterprise plan only.
- **Maintenance costs extra** — work orders and maintenance management are a paid add-on on every plan, priced per admin plus per operator.

**Best for:** Media production companies and university AV departments that need dedicated equipment checkout workflows and can justify the per-admin cost. Teams with a small number of administrators managing equipment for many users.

**Pricing:** Core $184/admin/mo → Business $275/admin/mo → Enterprise $367/admin/mo → E.L.A. (custom). Billed annually; every plan starts at 3 admins. Unlimited users and items on every plan. No free plan; 7-day free trial.

### Sortly

**What it is:** A visual inventory management app designed for simplicity. Sortly's strength is making it easy to photograph, categorize, and locate items — it's closer to an inventory catalog than an equipment checkout system.

**Strengths:**
- **Visual-first approach** — every item gets photos. The interface is clean and intuitive. Non-technical users love it.
- **Simple setup** — you can start cataloging items in minutes. Low learning curve.
- **Offline mobile access** — on every plan, including Free. Useful for field work.
- **Low stock alerts** — set minimums, get notified, from the Advanced plan. Good for consumables.
- **Free plan** — 100 items, 1 user.

**Limitations:**
- **No equipment booking or reservations listed** — Sortly's plan comparison lists check-in/check-out but no reservations or booking calendar. If you need to prevent double-bookings, look elsewhere.
- **Basic check-out** — check-in/check-out starts on the Advanced plan, and Sortly's help center describes checking an item out as moving it to a folder. Sortly's pages don't mention due dates, return reminders or an availability calendar.
- **No kits listed** — Sortly's pricing and feature pages don't mention kits or bundles, so plan on tracking a camera and its accessories as separate items.
- **Item and user caps on every plan** — you start at 100 items (free). Even on their $299/month Premium tier, you are strictly capped at 8 users and 5,000 items. If you need 10 people to just view inventory, you're forced into custom enterprise pricing.
- **Year-two price jump** — new yearly plans are 50% off in the first year only; after that the yearly discount is 20%. Advanced costs $288 for year one and about $470 a year after that.

**Best for:** Small businesses that need a simple, visual catalog of what they own and where it is. Homeowners tracking valuables. Teams where "what do we have?" is a bigger question than "who has it right now?"

**Pricing:** Free (100 items, 1 user) → Advanced $49/mo (500 items, 2 users) → Ultra $149/mo (2,000 items, 5 users) → Premium $299/mo (5,000 items, 8 users) → Enterprise (quote; 10,000+ items, 12+ users). New yearly plans are half price in year one, then 20% off. 14-day free trial on paid plans (card required; promotional offers excluded).

### Snipe-IT

**What it is:** An open-source IT asset management platform built for sysadmins. Snipe-IT tracks the full lifecycle of IT hardware — procurement, deployment, assignment, depreciation, and disposal. It has a large community and is used by thousands of IT departments.

**Strengths:**
- **Free and self-hosted** — the entire platform is free to run on your own server. No per-asset or per-user limits. This is genuinely free, not freemium.
- **Deep IT lifecycle features** — depreciation, license management, consumables tracking, component tracking. No other tool on this list matches its IT asset depth.
- **LDAP/Active Directory, SAML SSO and SCIM** — pulls users directly from your directory. Important for IT teams.
- **Kits** — pre-defined kits for faster checkouts.
- **Extensive API** — well-documented REST API for custom integrations.
- **Large community** — 15,000+ GitHub stars, active development, extensive documentation.

**Limitations:**
- **No booking or reservation system** — you can check an asset out to a user (with an optional signature on acceptance) and users can request assets marked as requestable, but you cannot reserve one for a future date. Snipe-IT's own README points to a third-party add-on for reservations. For shared equipment scheduling, this is a deal-breaker.
- **Free means running your own server** — Snipe-IT is web software with no installer; the free version runs on your own web server (Linux, Windows or Mac, or Docker). Hosting by the vendor starts at $39.99/month.
- **Mobile app still in beta** — the official app is in beta (TestFlight on iOS, Google Play on Android). Otherwise you use the mobile-friendly web UI.

**Best for:** IT departments managing laptops, servers, network gear, and software licenses. Organizations with technical staff who can self-host. Teams that need depreciation tracking and IT lifecycle reporting.

**Pricing:** Free (self-hosted, unlimited) → Hosted from $39.99/month (Basic) to $249.99/month (small dedicated server), unlimited users and assets. Larger dedicated servers are $5,000–$7,500 a year.

### EZOfficeInventory (EZO)

**What it is:** A mid-market asset tracking and maintenance platform that bridges equipment checkout and CMMS functionality. EZOfficeInventory is now sold as EZO EAM. It combines reservation workflows with maintenance scheduling and integrates with help desk tools like Zendesk and Jira.

**Strengths:**
- **Reservations + maintenance available** — reservations are on every plan, and one of the few platforms that combines equipment booking with maintenance. Preventive maintenance and work orders come through a CMMS add-on priced per admin user (or the Enterprise plan); basic service records start on Advanced.
- **Kits** — packages and bundles from the Advanced plan.
- **Integrations** — Zendesk, Jira, QuickBooks Online, Google Workspace and GPS, plus LDAP and SAML, on the Premium and Enterprise plans. Lower tiers don't include them.
- **Depreciation and compliance** — depreciation on every plan; audits from Advanced.
- **Native mobile apps** — iOS and Android with barcode scanning.
- **Roles and permissions** — team-based access controls from Advanced, advanced roles and permissions on Premium.
- **"Unlimited users"** — on all plans, subject to a fair-use policy.
- **14-day free trial** — no credit card required.

**Limitations:**
- **Per-item pricing that scales steeply** — Essential is $44.75/month for 100 items but $156.68/month for 1,500 items, billed yearly ($48 and $197.24 billed monthly). Premium at 1,500 items is $315.80/month billed yearly. Schools and large teams hit high costs fast.
- **Maintenance is a separate add-on** — preventive maintenance and work orders are not in Essential, Advanced or Premium. They need the CMMS add-on, priced per admin user, or the Enterprise plan.
- **Key integrations tier-locked** — Zendesk and Jira require Premium (from $57.50/month for just 100 items, billed yearly).
- **Offline mobile app is Enterprise-only** — Essential, Advanced and Premium include the mobile app, but offline mode is only on the custom-priced Enterprise plan.
- **Closed source** — no self-hosting, no code transparency.

**Best for:** Mid-market operations teams that need equipment checkout AND are willing to pay for maintenance add-ons. Teams already on Zendesk/Jira who can justify Premium tier pricing.

**Pricing:** Essential from $44.75/mo → Advanced from $52.25/mo → Premium from $57.50/mo (100 items, billed yearly) → Enterprise (custom). At 500 items it is $81.41 / $117.11 / $142.10 a month, billed yearly. Scales steeply with item count. The CMMS add-on is priced separately. Verify current pricing at [ezo.io](https://ezo.io/ezofficeinventory/pricing/).

### Asset Panda

**What it is:** A highly customizable asset management platform targeting mid-market and enterprise organizations. Asset Panda's core strength is deep configurability — custom fields, custom workflows, conditional forms, and approval chains for virtually any asset management scenario.

**Strengths:**
- **Deep customization** — custom fields, statuses, workflows, forms, and conditional logic. One of the most configurable platforms in the category.
- **Check-in/check-out with signatures** — signatures at pickup where your process needs them, condition photos, and a DocuSign integration.
- **Reservation system** — calendar and Gantt views, approval managers, and checks against overlapping bookings.
- **Maintenance** — work orders and recurring service with reminders.
- **Compliance and audit trails** — assignment, transfer and return history kept on each asset.
- **Depreciation tracking** — built-in fixed asset accounting.
- **Wide integrations** — Zendesk, Jira, ServiceNow, Slack, Intune, Jamf, DocuSign, Microsoft Entra ID and Okta.
- **Strong education positioning** — dedicated K-12 and higher-ed pages with customer stories.

**Limitations:**
- **Pricing on request only** — the pricing page shows no prices. You get a custom quote, and their team sets up a 7-day trial for you. You can't compare cost without talking to sales.
- **No self-serve start** — the quote form books a call, and their team sets up your trial account. You can't sign up and try it on your own the same day.
- **No kits listed** — Asset Panda's site doesn't mention kits or bundles, so ask how they handle gear that goes out together.
- **Closed source** — no self-hosting, no code transparency.

**Best for:** Enterprise organizations with complex compliance requirements, dedicated asset management staff, and budget for a custom-quoted contract. Government, healthcare, and large institutions where configurability justifies the cost.

**Pricing:** Custom quote only; no prices published. 7-day free trial, set up by their team. Contact their sales team for current pricing.

### Reftab

**What it is:** An IT-focused asset tracking tool that has expanded beyond basic inventory into reservations, kits, maintenance and software tracking. Reftab has a free plan for 50 assets and a flat $125/month Hardware plan with unlimited users — a pricing model that works well for teams with many people.

**Strengths:**
- **Unlimited users on the Hardware plan** — no per-seat fees, and the free plan lists no user limit. This is a genuine differentiator for teams where many people need access.
- **Reservations and calendar** — equipment booking with calendar views, included on all plans including free.
- **Kit templates** — pre-define the parts of a kit and check them out together. The pricing page doesn't say which plans include them.
- **LDAP sync and SAML SSO** — LDAP adds directory users as borrowers; SSO works with Microsoft Entra ID, Okta and ADFS.
- **Native mobile apps** — iOS and Android apps for scanning and asset management.
- **Maintenance and work orders** — on the Hardware plan ($125/month).
- **Depreciation and software tracking** — straight-line depreciation, plus a separate Software Asset Management plan.
- **API access** — public API for paying customers.
- **Free tier** — 50 assets with reservations, calendars, and mobile app access.

**Limitations:**
- **Asset count still matters past the free plan** — Free covers 50 assets. The pricing page doesn't say how many assets the $125/month Hardware plan covers, and Complete ITAM is custom-priced on assets plus software users. Accessories and licenses don't count as assets. If you track thousands of items, ask for a quote before you commit. Shelf doesn't charge by asset count.
- **IT-first product** — Reftab sells itself as IT asset management, and its paid plans center on IT work: Intune and Jamf integrations, onboarding and offboarding tasks, software license tracking.
- **Closed source** — no self-hosting option.

**Best for:** Teams with many users but moderate asset counts. IT departments that need reservations, kits, and maintenance in one tool without per-seat fees.

**Pricing:** Free (50 assets) → Hardware Asset Management $125/mo (unlimited users) → Complete ITAM (custom, based on assets + software users). Software Asset Management on its own is $9 per tracked software user per year. 14-day free trial on paid plans. Verify at [reftab.com/pricing](https://www.reftab.com/pricing).

## Which Tool Fits Your Situation?

![Shelf dashboard showing equipment status and bookings](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/blog/6584589239f5291e33fe59cf_dashboard-2.jpg)

### If you're a school or university

Your priorities: tight budget, many users (students + staff), equipment booking to prevent double-bookings, minimal training requirement, compliance documentation for procurement.

<SummaryBox title="Best Fit for Education">

- **Best overall:** Shelf — unlimited users on flat pricing, QR-first workflows students adopt immediately, booking with conflict prevention, VPAT/508 compliance docs, [education-specific content](/industries/education)
- **If you need higher-ed AV checkout specifically:** Cheqroom — purpose-built for media/AV rooms, university case studies, native mobile app on iOS and Android
- **If you need IT device tracking (Chromebooks, laptops):** Snipe-IT — free self-hosted, LDAP integration, depreciation tracking. But no booking system.
- **If budget allows and you need maximum configurability:** Asset Panda — K-12 and higher-ed pages, Microsoft Entra ID and Okta integrations, compliance features. But pricing is by custom quote only.

</SummaryBox>

### If you're a media or production company

Your priorities: booking shared gear (cameras, audio, lighting), kit management (camera + lenses + batteries), preventing double-bookings, custody accountability for expensive equipment.

<SummaryBox title="Best Fit for Media & Production">

- **Best overall:** Shelf or Cheqroom — both have booking, kits, and custody. Shelf wins on pricing (flat vs per-admin), open source, and a native iOS + Android Companion app. Cheqroom wins on AV-specific heritage.
- **Avoid:** Sortly (no booking listed), Snipe-IT (no built-in booking)
- **If you also need maintenance tracking:** EZOfficeInventory — combines reservations with CMMS (maintenance is a paid add-on)

</SummaryBox>

### If you're a field operations team

Your priorities: tool tracking across job sites and trucks, rugged mobile workflows, accountability for equipment handoffs between crews, low training overhead.

<SummaryBox title="Best Fit for Field Operations">

- **Best overall:** Shelf — QR scanning works on any phone (browser or the free [Shelf Companion app](/mobile-app) on iPhone and Android), custody chain for crew handoffs, location tracking across sites, flat pricing for entire crew.
- **If you need maintenance scheduling:** EZOfficeInventory — combines asset tracking with maintenance (available as a paid add-on)
- **If construction tool tracking is the core need:** Check our [tool tracking](/solutions/tool-tracking) page for the specific workflow

</SummaryBox>

### If you're an IT department

Your priorities: tracking hardware lifecycle (procurement → deployment → depreciation → disposal), license management, LDAP/AD integration, compliance reporting.

<SummaryBox title="Best Fit for IT">

- **Best for IT lifecycle management:** Snipe-IT — free, open source, depreciation, licenses, consumables, LDAP. The most complete IT asset tool on this list.
- **If you need both IT tracking and equipment checkout:** Shelf for the operational layer (checkout, booking) + Snipe-IT for the lifecycle layer. Some teams use both.
- **If you need help desk integration:** EZO (Zendesk and Jira on Premium) or Asset Panda (Zendesk, Jira and ServiceNow)
- **For small IT teams wanting simplicity with reservations:** Reftab — free for 50 assets, $125/month for the Hardware plan with unlimited users; booking calendar, LDAP sync, SAML SSO, mobile apps

</SummaryBox>

### If you're a small business

Your priorities: simplicity, low cost, fast setup, answer the question "what do we have and where is it?"

<SummaryBox title="Best Fit for Small Business">

- **Simplest visual inventory:** Sortly — photo-first, intuitive, works immediately. Best for cataloging what you own. Check-in/check-out starts on the paid Advanced plan, and no booking is listed.
- **If you need checkout/booking too:** Shelf — free plan covers unlimited assets for one user. Upgrade only when you need the team features.
- **If you're still using spreadsheets:** Read our [spreadsheet vs. Shelf comparison](/alternatives/spreadsheets) — it covers exactly when spreadsheets stop working and what the minimum viable upgrade looks like.

</SummaryBox>

## What We'd Choose If We Didn't Make Shelf

If we were evaluating these tools as a buyer — not a competitor — here's where we'd land:

## How to Decide

<Callout type="takeaway">

Before you trial anything, answer four questions:

1. **Do you need booking/reservations?** If yes, your options are Shelf, Cheqroom, EZO, Asset Panda, or Reftab. Sortly doesn't list it, and Snipe-IT needs a third-party add-on.
2. **How many people need access?** If it's more than 10, user caps (Sortly tops out at 8 users before Enterprise) and per-admin pricing (Cheqroom at $184+ per admin, 3-admin minimum) add up fast. Flat pricing (Shelf), unlimited users (Reftab, EZO), and free self-hosting (Snipe-IT) don't penalize team size.
3. **How much time do you have to set this up?** Shelf, Sortly, Reftab, EZO and Cheqroom let you start a free plan or trial on your own today. Asset Panda's team sets up your trial with you after a call, and Snipe-IT's free version needs your own server. If you need to be tracking equipment by Friday, start with the tools you can sign up for today.
4. **What's your technical capacity?** If you have a sysadmin who can manage a server, Snipe-IT's free self-hosting is compelling. If you want zero setup, a cloud-hosted SaaS (Shelf, Cheqroom, Sortly, EZO) is the right path.

</Callout>

Every tool on this list offers a free plan or a free trial — Cheqroom, EZO and Asset Panda have trials only, so a free plan is only possible with Shelf, Sortly, Reftab or self-hosted Snipe-IT. Don't choose based on a comparison page — including this one. Try the 2-3 tools that fit your situation and decide based on your own experience.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is equipment management software?

Equipment management software keeps a record of the equipment an organization owns and tracks who has each item, where it is, when it is booked and when it needs a check. Most tools use QR codes or barcodes scanned with a phone. Some add bookings and kits for shared gear, others add maintenance work orders.

### What is the difference between equipment management software and a CMMS?

Equipment management software is built around equipment that people use and pass around: check-out, custody, bookings and kits. A CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) is built around keeping machines running: work orders, preventive maintenance schedules, parts and technicians. Some tools cover parts of both; the comparison table above shows which.

### Is there free equipment management software?

Yes. Shelf has a free plan with unlimited assets for one user, Sortly and Reftab have limited free plans, and Snipe-IT is free if you host it yourself. Free plans are usually limited by users or items, so check those limits against your team size.

### How much does equipment management software cost?

It depends mostly on the pricing model. Tools charge per user, per admin, per item or one flat price for the workspace. Shelf's Team plan is $67 a month or $370 a year for unlimited users. The comparison table above lists the other tools' published prices with the date they were checked.

### What features does equipment management software need?

At minimum: a way to identify each item (QR code or barcode), a record of who has it, and a search that shows where things are. Shared equipment also needs bookings with conflict checks and kits. Teams that maintain machines need service reminders or work orders. Audits and reports show what is missing and what sits unused.
