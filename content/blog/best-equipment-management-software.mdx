**Our verdict: Shelf is the best equipment management software for teams that share and check out gear** — schools and universities, media and AV teams, IT loaner pools and tool rooms. The Team plan is one flat price, $370 a year or $67 a month, with unlimited users, so every student, crew member or technician can have their own login. It includes bookings with conflict checks, reports, kits on every plan, and the free Shelf Companion app for iPhone and Android. If you need fixed-asset depreciation, help-desk connectors or a full maintenance system, another tool on this list fits better, and we say which one below.

**Equipment management software** is a system that keeps a record of every piece of equipment an organization owns and tracks who has it, where it is, when it is booked and when it is due for a check. It replaces sign-out sheets and spreadsheets, so shared gear stops getting lost, double-booked or skipped for service.

**What to look for in equipment management software:**
- **Scanning anyone can do:** QR codes or barcodes read with a phone camera. RFID and GPS tags help in some settings, but cost more per item.
- **Check-out and custody:** a record of who has each item, since when, and when it is due back.
- **Bookings:** reservations for future dates that block double-bookings, if many people share the same gear.
- **Kits:** items that travel together go out and come back as one unit.
- **Maintenance:** reminders for inspections and service; work orders if you run a full maintenance program.
- **Audits and reports:** scan-based stock checks, utilization, and overdue items.
- **A pricing model that fits your team:** per user, per admin, per item or flat per workspace makes a large difference as you grow.

We built Shelf, so read this page knowing that. Every competitor fact below comes from that vendor's own website or pricing page, checked on 3 November 2026, and we name the cases where another tool is the better choice.

## Three Categories, Not One

Before comparing tools, it helps to understand that "[equipment management software](/solutions/equipment-management)" means different things to different people. The market breaks into three categories:

**1. Equipment checkout and booking tools** — built for tracking who has what, scheduling shared gear, and preventing double-bookings. Best for schools, media production, shared equipment pools.
*Tools: Shelf, Cheqroom*

**2. Asset inventory and registry tools** — built for cataloging what you own, where it is, and its lifecycle stage. Best for IT departments, fixed asset tracking, compliance.
*Tools: Snipe-IT, Reftab, Sortly*

**3. Maintenance and operations platforms (CMMS)** — built for scheduling maintenance, tracking work orders, and managing equipment health. Best for facilities, manufacturing, fleet management.
*Tools: EZOfficeInventory (bridges categories 2 and 3), Asset Panda (bridges categories 1 and 2)*

If you need equipment booking and checkout, Shelf is built for exactly that, and comparing it to a CMMS tool is apples to oranges. If full preventive maintenance with work orders is your primary need, you need a tool in category 3. Knowing your category narrows the field immediately.

## The Tools

### Shelf

**What it is:** An open-source equipment management platform built around QR code scanning, equipment booking, and custody tracking. Shelf is what we make — and it is the tool we recommend for any team that shares and checks out gear.

**Why Shelf is the best fit for shared gear:**
- **One flat price for the whole team** — the Team plan is $370 a year or $67 a month with unlimited users and unlimited assets. No per-seat, per-admin or per-item fees, so adding 200 students costs the same as adding 2.
- **Bookings with conflict checks** — reserve gear for future dates on a calendar. Items already booked for that time are marked "Already booked", so double-bookings don't happen.
- **Kits on every plan** — bundle a camera with its lens, battery, and SD card, even on the free plan. When you scan a booking back in, any kit item not yet returned stays listed as pending.
- **Reports included** — ready-to-run reports on bookings, assets and custody (overdue items, utilization, idle assets and more), on the Plus and Team plans. Every report exports to CSV; chart reports also export to PDF.
- **Free iOS and Android app** — the Shelf Companion app on the App Store and Google Play covers scanning, audits, custody handoffs and bookings. The web app also works in any phone browser.
- **Open source you can self-host** — the web app is AGPL-3.0 and the code is public. You can host it yourself with the community-maintained Docker setup. No vendor lock-in.
- **A free plan you can actually use** — unlimited assets for one user. Not a trial, not a demo.
- **QR-first workflow** — scanning is the main action, not data entry. Students, staff, and field workers pick it up without training.

**Where another tool fits better:**
- **Depreciation and fixed-asset accounting** — Shelf tracks how equipment is used, not its book value. If you need depreciation, choose Snipe-IT, EZO, Asset Panda or Reftab, or keep depreciation in your finance system next to Shelf.
- **Help-desk connectors** — Shelf has no Zendesk, Jira, or ServiceNow connectors. EZO (Zendesk, Jira) and Asset Panda (Zendesk, Jira, ServiceNow) have them.
- **Full CMMS** — Shelf has asset reminders (one-time on every plan, repeating on Plus and Team), and each reminder it sends is noted in the asset's activity. You record the service itself in notes and custom fields. There are no work orders, parts inventory or technician dispatching. If maintenance is your main job, choose EZO, Asset Panda or a dedicated maintenance platform.
- **The mobile app is a field client** — setting up audits, bulk imports, custom fields, users and roles, reports, and integrations happen in the web app.

**Pricing:** Free (1 user, unlimited assets) → Plus $34/mo (1 user, adds reports and repeating reminders) → Team $67/mo or $370/yr (unlimited users, adds bookings) → Enterprise (custom). [See pricing →](/pricing)

### Cheqroom

**What it is:** An equipment management platform from the AV and media world. Cheqroom handles equipment checkout, booking, and kits, priced per admin.

**Choose Cheqroom only if you need:**
- Add-ons Shelf doesn't have: RFID, room management, and work orders priced per admin plus per operator.

- A vendor rooted in AV and media, with a higher-education page and university customer stories.

### Sortly

**What it is:** A visual inventory app designed for simplicity. Sortly makes it easy to photograph, categorize, and locate items — it's closer to an inventory catalog than an equipment checkout system.

**Where Shelf is better than Sortly:**
- **No user caps** — Sortly allows 1, 2, 5 or 8 users depending on the plan; a team of 10 needs Enterprise, which is quote only. Shelf's Team plan has unlimited users for $370 a year.
- **No item caps** — Sortly's plans stop at 100, 500, 2,000 or 5,000 items. Shelf has unlimited assets on every plan, including free.
- **Bookings** — Sortly's plan comparison lists check-in/check-out but no reservations or booking calendar. Shelf has bookings with conflict checks.
- **Kits** — Sortly's pricing and feature pages don't mention kits or bundles. Shelf has kits on every plan.
- **Same price every year** — new Sortly yearly plans are 50% off in year one only; after that the yearly discount is 20%, so Advanced goes from $288 in year one to about $470 a year. Shelf's yearly price has no first-year-only discount.

**Choose Sortly only if you need:**
- A photo-first stock catalog with purchase orders (Ultra and up).
- QuickBooks Online (Premium) or Amazon Business integrations.
- Offline mobile access, which Sortly includes on every plan.

**Pricing:** Free (100 items, 1 user) → Advanced $49/mo (500 items, 2 users) → Ultra $149/mo (2,000 items, 5 users) → Premium $299/mo (5,000 items, 8 users) → Enterprise (quote; 10,000+ items, 12+ users). New yearly plans are half price in year one, then 20% off. 14-day free trial on paid plans (card required; promotional offers excluded).

### Snipe-IT

**What it is:** An open-source IT asset management platform built for sysadmins. Snipe-IT tracks the full lifecycle of IT hardware — procurement, assignment, depreciation, and disposal — and has a large community (15,000+ GitHub stars).

**Where Shelf is better than Snipe-IT:**
- **Bookings** — Snipe-IT can check an asset out to a user and let users request assets, but you cannot reserve one for a future date; its own README points to a third-party add-on for reservations. Shelf has bookings with conflict checks built in.
- **A finished mobile app** — Snipe-IT's official app is in beta (TestFlight on iOS, Google Play on Android). Shelf Companion is live on the App Store and Google Play.
- **Hosted price** — Snipe-IT's Basic Hosting is $399.99 a year; Shelf's Team plan is $370 a year and includes bookings.
- **No server needed** — Snipe-IT's free version runs on your own web server. Shelf's free plan runs in the cloud, and you can still self-host if you want to.

**Choose Snipe-IT only if you need:**
- Depreciation, software license tracking, consumables and components — a deeper IT lifecycle than Shelf offers.
- Active Directory/LDAP login and sync, and the 25 built-in MDM/RMM sync adapters.

**Pricing:** Free (self-hosted, unlimited) → Hosted from $39.99/month (Basic) to $249.99/month (small dedicated server), unlimited users and assets. Larger dedicated servers are $5,000–$7,500 a year.

## When We'd Point You to Another Tool

Shelf is the right choice when your core problem is shared equipment: who has it, when it's booked, and whether it came back complete. When the core problem is something else, we'd choose differently:

- **IT lifecycle with depreciation and licenses:** Snipe-IT. It's free to self-host and its IT depth is the strongest on this list.
- **Preventive maintenance with work orders:** EZO with its CMMS add-on, or Asset Panda.
- **Stock and consumables catalog:** Sortly.
- **Software license tracking:** Reftab's software asset management plan.

For checkout, booking, kits and custody across a team of any size, we think Shelf is the best option — and the cost table shows it is also the lowest published price.

## How to Decide

<Callout type="takeaway">

Before you trial anything, answer four questions:

1. **Do you need booking/reservations?** If yes, your options are Shelf, Cheqroom, EZO, Asset Panda, or Reftab. Sortly doesn't list it, and Snipe-IT needs a third-party add-on. Of those five, Shelf is the only one with a published price under $400 a year for a team of 10.
2. **How many people need access?** If it's more than 10, user caps (Sortly tops out at 8 users before Enterprise) and per-admin pricing (Cheqroom at $184+ per admin, 3-admin minimum) add up fast. ### What is equipment management software?

Equipment management software keeps a record of the equipment an organization owns and tracks who has each item, where it is, when it is booked and when it needs a check. Most tools use QR codes or barcodes scanned with a phone. Some add bookings and kits for shared gear, others add maintenance work orders.

### What is the difference between equipment management software and a CMMS?

Equipment management software is built around equipment that people use and pass around: check-out, custody, bookings and kits. A CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) is built around keeping machines running: work orders, preventive maintenance schedules, parts and technicians. Some tools cover parts of both; the comparison table above shows which.

### Is there free equipment management software?

Yes. Shelf has a free plan with unlimited assets for one user, Sortly and Reftab have limited free plans (100 items and 50 assets), and Snipe-IT and Shelf are free if you host them yourself. Free plans are usually limited by users or items, so check those limits against your team size.

### How much does equipment management software cost?

It depends mostly on the pricing model. Tools charge per user, per admin, per item or one flat price for the workspace. Shelf's Team plan is $67 a month or $370 a year for unlimited users. The cost table at the top of this page shows what a team of 10 with 500 items pays at each vendor's published prices.

### What features does equipment management software need?

At minimum: a way to identify each item (QR code or barcode), a record of who has it, and a search that shows where things are. Shared equipment also needs bookings with conflict checks and kits. Teams that maintain machines need service reminders or work orders. Audits and reports show what is missing and what sits unused.
