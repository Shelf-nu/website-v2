**IT asset management (ITAM) software** keeps a record of every device an organization owns (laptops, monitors, phones, docks and the accessories around them) and of who has each one, where it is, what it cost and when it should be replaced. Some tools also find devices on the network by themselves and track software licenses.

**What a small IT team should look for:**
- **How devices get into the system:** typed in or imported from a spreadsheet, or found automatically by a network scan, an agent installed on each device, or a sync with your device management (MDM) tool such as Intune or Jamf.
- **Hand-outs and returns:** a record of who has each laptop, since when, and what has to come back when someone leaves.
- **Loaner bookings:** reservations for future dates, if you run a pool of spare laptops.
- **Accessories:** chargers, docks and headsets either grouped with the laptop or counted as stock.
- **Software licenses:** seats, renewals and who uses what, if that is part of your job.
- **Sign-in and directory:** single sign-on (SSO) and user sync with Entra ID, Google Workspace or Okta.
- **A price that fits 100 to 2,000 devices:** per device, per user, per technician (agent) or flat per workspace makes a large difference at this size.

Most "best ITAM software" lists are written by one of the vendors, who rank themselves first on every line. We make Shelf, so we have a perspective too. But several tools below do things Shelf does not do at all, such as network discovery and license tracking, and we say so plainly. The goal is to help you pick the right tool for your team, even if it is not ours.

## Two Kinds of IT Asset Tool

Small IT teams usually end up choosing between two kinds of tool, and knowing which one you need removes half the list.

**1. Discovery and service desk suites** find devices on your network or through an agent, record installed software, and often sit next to a ticketing system. They answer "what is on our network and what is installed on it?"
*Tools: Lansweeper, Freshservice*

**2. Asset registers with check-out** hold a record for every device, label it with a QR code or barcode, and track who has it, where it is and when it is due back. They answer "who has laptop 214 and is it coming back?" Several of them also sync from Intune or Jamf so you do not type devices in by hand.
*Tools: Snipe-IT, Reftab, EZO, AssetTiger, Asset Panda, itemit, Shelf, and Sortly (an inventory app that some small teams use for devices)*

Many teams use one of each: discovery for the network, a register for the physical hand-outs. If you mainly need to know what software is installed where, start with the first group. If you mainly lose track of who has which laptop, charger and loaner, start with the second.

## The Tools

### Shelf

**What it is:** An open-source asset tracking platform built around QR labels, check-out and custody, loaner bookings and kits. Shelf is what we make, so read this section with that in mind. It covers the physical side of IT: devices handed to people, lent out and returned.

**Strengths:**
- **One flat price for unlimited users and assets:** the Team plan is $67 a month or $370 a year for the whole workspace. Staff who borrow loaners can have their own sign-in without adding cost.
- **Hand-outs and returns with history:** every device records who has it and since when. When someone leaves, you can list everything assigned to them and release each item as it comes back. Custody is on every plan, including Free.
- **Loaner bookings:** reserve a laptop for set dates and Shelf blocks overlapping bookings. Bookings are on the Team plan. See [Bookings](/features/bookings).
- **Kits and accessory stock:** group a laptop with its charger and dock as a [kit](/features/kits) on any plan. Track interchangeable chargers, dongles and cables [by quantity](/features/consumables-tracking), with a low-stock email to the owner and admins when stock runs low.
- **Phone scanning:** the free Shelf Companion app is on the App Store and Google Play for scanning, custody and booking check-in and check-out. See the [mobile app](/mobile-app).
- **Warranty and refresh reminders:** store a warranty end date as a custom field and set an [asset reminder](/features/asset-reminders) for it. Repeating reminders are on Plus, Team and Enterprise.
- **Open source:** the code is public under the AGPL license. See [open-source asset management](/solutions/open-source-asset-management).

**Limitations: where other tools are better for IT:**
- **No network discovery or agents.** Shelf does not scan your network or install anything on devices. You add devices by [CSV import](/knowledge-base/importing-assets-to-shelf-csv-guide) (Plus plan and up) or by hand. Lansweeper and Freshservice do discovery.
- **No MDM sync.** There is no Intune, Jamf or Kandji connection, so a new device in your MDM does not appear in Shelf by itself. Snipe-IT, Reftab and Asset Panda list these syncs.
- **No software license tracking.** Shelf tracks physical things, not seats or subscriptions. Snipe-IT, Reftab's software plan and Freshservice Pro do this.
- **No help desk integration.** There is no Jira, Zendesk or ServiceNow connector.
- **No depreciation.** Shelf stores what a device is worth, but does not calculate depreciation. Snipe-IT, AssetTiger, itemit and Asset Panda do.
- **No public API.** Shelf does not publish an API for your own scripts or other tools.
- **Self-hosting takes work.** Self-hosting uses a community-maintained Docker setup and needs your own Supabase instance. Snipe-IT's self-hosted version is the more established route.
- **Paid add-ons for IT basics:** SSO (SAML, with SCIM user provisioning included) is an add-on on the Team plan, priced per person who signs in with SSO, from $9 per user per month on annual billing for the first 15 users. Scan [audits](/features/audits) are an add-on at $37 a month or $205 a year. Keeping existing barcode labels needs the [Alternative Barcodes](/knowledge-base/alternative-barcodes) add-on.

**Best for:** Small IT teams whose real problem is physical: who has each laptop, which loaners are out, and whether the charger came back. It also suits teams that track non-IT equipment in the same place.

**Pricing:** Free (1 user, unlimited assets) → Plus $34/mo or $190/yr (1 user, adds CSV import, reports and repeating reminders) → Team $67/mo or $370/yr (unlimited users, bookings) → Enterprise (custom). [See pricing →](/pricing)

### Snipe-IT

**What it is:** The best-known open-source IT asset management system, built for IT departments. Its own README describes it as knowing who has which laptop, when it was purchased so it can be depreciated, and handling software licenses.

**Strengths:**
- **Free to self-host, with no asset or user limits:** the pricing page lists the self-hosted version as free with unlimited users and assets, and the code is AGPL-3.0 on GitHub.
- **Deep IT lifecycle features:** license management, depreciation, accessories, consumables and components, plus audits and asset history.
- **MDM and directory sync:** the features page lists 20 sync adapters, including Microsoft Intune, Jamf, Kandji, Google Workspace and NinjaOne, plus Active Directory/LDAP sync, SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning.
- **REST API and integrations:** a documented JSON REST API, a Jira integration, and Slack and Teams notifications.
- **Kits:** predefined kits for faster check-outs.
- **Hosted option:** if you do not want to run a server, Basic Hosting is $39.99/mo or $399.99/yr with unlimited users and assets.

**Limitations:**
- **No calendar reservations:** assets can be marked "requestable" so users ask for them, but the features page does not list future-date bookings. Loaner pools with set dates need a workaround or a community add-on.
- **Self-hosting is yours to run:** the free version comes with community support only. Email support starts with paid hosting.
- **Mobile app still in beta:** the official mobile app is in beta (TestFlight on iOS, Google Play on Android), and third-party apps fill the gap.

**Best for:** IT teams with someone who can run a server, or with $400 a year for hosting, who want licenses, depreciation and MDM sync in one free or low-cost tool.

**Pricing:** Self-hosted free → Basic Hosting $39.99/mo or $399.99/yr → Small Business Hosting $99.99/mo or $999.99/yr → Dedicated Hosting $249.99/mo or $2,499.99/yr. Sources: [pricing](https://snipeitapp.com/pricing), [features](https://snipeitapp.com/features), [README](https://github.com/grokability/snipe-it), as of 2026-10-29.

### Reftab

**What it is:** A cloud IT asset management tool with separate plans for hardware and for software. It charges by assets (and by software users for license tracking), with unlimited users.

**Strengths:**
- **Free for up to 50 assets, with no time limit,** including reservations with calendars, check-out, barcodes and QR codes, and the iOS and Android apps.
- **MDM and help desk integrations on the hardware plan:** Intune, Jamf, Iru and Jira, plus "30+ more", along with audits, maintenance and work orders, and reservations.
- **Unlimited users** on every plan.
- **Software asset management as its own plan:** SaaS and installed-software discovery, license and user tracking, and spend, at $9 per tracked software user per year.
- **Accessories and licenses do not count as assets,** and archived assets do not count toward the quota, according to the pricing FAQ.

**Limitations:**
- **The hardware price does not say how many assets it covers.** The page shows $125/mo but no asset count, so you need to ask for a quote at 1,000 or 2,000 devices.
- **Hardware plus software is custom-priced** ("Complete ITAM").
- **Cloud only:** no self-hosted option is listed.

**Best for:** Small IT teams that want MDM sync, reservations and unlimited users in a cloud tool, and may add license tracking later.

**Pricing:** Free (50 assets) → Hardware Asset Management $125/mo → Software Asset Management $9 per tracked software user per year → Complete ITAM (custom). 14-day trial on paid plans. Source: [reftab.com/pricing](https://www.reftab.com/pricing), as of 2026-10-29.

### Lansweeper

**What it is:** An IT discovery platform. It scans your network, cloud and operational technology (OT), and can use an agent, to build an inventory of every device and what runs on it.

**Strengths:**
- **Discovery is the product:** IT, OT, cloud and agent discovery, plus connectors that import from SCCM, Intune, Airwatch and G-Suite MDM, and automatic warranty collection.
- **Unlimited user seats** on every plan, and SSO from Starter.
- **Free tier:** after a 14-day full trial, you can continue free for up to 100 assets.

**Limitations:**
- **Built to find devices, not to lend them:** the pricing page lists no check-out, custody or loaner reservations.
- **Priced for larger estates:** Starter is from €270/mo billed annually and includes 2,000 assets. Starter only connects to service desks for ticket enrichment; other integrations need Pro.
- **Full API access is Enterprise only.**

**Best for:** IT teams that need to know everything on the network, including devices nobody registered, and can pair it with a register for hand-outs.

## How to Decide

<Callout type="takeaway">

Before you trial anything, answer four questions:

1. **How do devices get into the system?** If you want them to appear by themselves, you need discovery (Lansweeper, Freshservice) or an MDM sync (Snipe-IT, Reftab, Asset Panda). If a CSV import and a QR label per device is fine, every tool on this page works.
2. **Do you track software licenses?** If yes, your shortlist is Snipe-IT, Reftab's software plan or Freshservice Pro. Shelf does not track licenses.
3. **Who needs to sign in?** If staff outside IT book loaners or confirm hand-outs, rule out tools with low user caps (Sortly, itemit Starter) and per-technician pricing for those users. Shelf Team, Snipe-IT, Reftab, EZO, AssetTiger and Lansweeper all list unlimited users.
4. **Who runs it?** Self-hosting Snipe-IT is free but someone has to maintain the server. Everything else here is a hosted service.

</Callout>

Every tool on this page offers a free plan or a trial; Asset Panda's is the shortest at 7 days. Trial the two or three that match your answers with 20 real devices, a few hand-outs and one return, and decide on that.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is IT asset management software?

IT asset management software keeps a record of the devices an organization owns, such as laptops, monitors and phones, and of who has each one, where it is, what it cost and when it should be replaced. Some tools add network discovery, software license tracking or a help desk.

### What is the difference between IT asset tracking and IT asset management?

IT asset tracking follows physical devices: who has them, where they are and whether they came back. IT asset management is broader and also covers purchasing, contracts, software licenses, depreciation and disposal, often with discovery agents. Shelf does the tracking side; Snipe-IT, Reftab and Freshservice also cover licenses.

### Is there free IT asset management software?

Yes. Snipe-IT is free if you host it yourself, with no asset or user limits. Shelf is free for one user with unlimited assets. Reftab is free for up to 50 assets, Sortly for 100 items and one user, and Lansweeper for up to 100 assets after its trial. AssetTiger now offers a 30-day trial rather than a free plan.

### How much does IT asset management software cost for a small team?

It depends on the pricing model. For 1,000 devices at published prices on 2026-10-29: Shelf Team is $370 a year for unlimited users, Snipe-IT hosting is $399.99 a year, AssetTiger Core is $40 a month, and Lansweeper Starter is from €270 a month billed annually. Asset Panda and EZO above 500 items are quote-based.

### Do I need network discovery for a small IT team?

Not always. Discovery finds devices and software on your network without typing them in, which matters if devices appear without IT knowing. If every device goes through your MDM, a register that syncs from Intune or Jamf may be enough. If you hand devices out from a stockroom, a CSV import and QR labels also work.

### Can Shelf replace Snipe-IT?

For hand-outs, loaner bookings, kits and accessory stock, yes. For software licenses, depreciation, MDM sync or an API, no: Snipe-IT does those and Shelf does not. See [Shelf vs Snipe-IT](/alternatives/snipe-it) for the full comparison.
