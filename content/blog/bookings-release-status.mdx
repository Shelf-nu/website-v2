## Bookings is live 📆

This page tracked the build-out of [Bookings](/features/bookings), one of our most requested features. That build is done. Bookings has left beta, is generally available on **Team** and **Enterprise** plans, and has kept growing since the first release.

### What you get today

- **Reserve assets for a date range**, with a custodian, a description, and tags.
- **Real-time availability**, so an asset that is already reserved, in custody, or out on another booking cannot be double-booked.
- **A calendar view** across the workspace, plus daily, weekly, and monthly availability views.
- **Kit-aware reservations**: book a kit and every asset inside it travels with the booking.
- **[Book by model](/features/bookings#book-by-model)**: reserve a count of an [Asset Model](/glossary/asset-models), such as "4x HDMI cable", and decide which physical units go out at pickup by scanning them.
- **Quantity-tracked reservations** for stock you [track by quantity](/features/consumables-tracking) rather than as individual units.
- **Progressive check-out and check-in**, so a booking can go out and come back in parts rather than all at once.
- **Automated check-out and check-in email reminders**, with configurable notification recipients per booking.
- **A subscribable [calendar feed](/knowledge-base/subscribe-your-calendar-to-shelf-bookings)** so bookings show up in Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar.
- **[CSV export](/knowledge-base/bookings-how-to-export-booking-information-and-assets-checked-out)** of bookings and the assets checked out on them, including per-asset check-in status.
- **Bookings on the phone** through the free [Shelf Companion app](/mobile-app) for iPhone and Android: create, edit, reserve, and check equipment in or out from the floor.
