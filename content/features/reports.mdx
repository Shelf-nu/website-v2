# Reports

Reports turn the day-to-day data Shelf already collects — bookings, custody changes, asset activity — into answers. Pick a report, set a timeframe, optionally filter by category or location, and read the result. No SQL, no spreadsheet exports to wrangle, no separate BI tool.

![Reports index in Shelf showing the grid of ready-to-run reports grouped by category — Bookings, Assets, Custody and Audits](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/reports.webp)

## What You Can Report On

Reports cover the parts of the workflow operations managers are usually asked about: who is overdue, what gets booked, what sits idle, how things are distributed across the inventory, and whether audits are getting done.

### Booking Reports

- **Booking Compliance** — On-time returns, late returns, and overdue items across the timeframe you pick. Lateness is measured against the deadline the booking was agreed to, so extending a booking does not clear a late return from the record. Filter by status, custodian, location, category, asset model, or a custom field.
- **Top Booked Assets** — The most frequently booked assets and their utilization patterns. Useful for capacity planning and replacement decisions.
- **Top Booked Kits** — The most frequently booked kits, ranked by how often each kit goes out. A kit is counted once per booking it appears in, so multiple assets from the same kit in one booking collapse to a single kit-booking. Useful when you bundle and lend equipment as kits and want to plan kit-level capacity.
- **Monthly Booking Trends** — Booking volume over time with month-over-month comparisons.
- **Overdue Items** — A live view of every booking that is currently past its end date. Filter by custodian or location to chase down what is missing right now.

### Asset Reports

- **Asset Inventory** — A complete snapshot of the inventory with category, location, and status filters. The fastest way to produce a list for an insurance audit or board report.
- **Asset Activity Summary** — Activity history across all assets — changes, custody handovers, and bookings.
- **Asset Utilization** — How effectively each asset is being used, based on booking and custody time.
- **Idle Assets** — Assets that have not been booked or checked out recently. Surfaces equipment that may be candidates for redistribution or retirement.
- **Asset Distribution** — Breakdown by category, location, status, and asset model for inventory planning.

### Custody Reports

- **Custody Snapshot** — A live view of every asset currently in custody and the team member it is assigned to.

### Audit Reports

- **Audit Completion** — Every audit created in the timeframe, with the completion rate as the headline and the number of overdue audits and assets found missing beside it. Each row carries the audit's expected, found, missing and unexpected counts, an accuracy bar, who created it, and when it started, was due and was completed; click a row to open the audit. The report reads from audit sessions, so it fills up for workspaces using the [Audits](/features/audits) add-on.

## How Reports Work

### Pick a Report

The reports index lists every report grouped by category — bookings, assets, custody, audits. Each card describes what the report shows; click in to run it.

### Set the Timeframe

Time-based reports include a timeframe picker with presets for the last 7 / 30 / 90 days, this or last month, this quarter, this year, all time, or a custom range. Snapshot reports (Asset Inventory, Custody Snapshot, Overdue Items) reflect the current state of the workspace and do not need a timeframe. Audit Completion counts an audit in the period it was created in, whatever happened to it since.

### Filter to the Slice You Care About

Every booking, asset and custody report except Top Booked Kits can be filtered from a filter bar with the filters that fit its data: category, location, status and custodian where they apply, plus **asset model** and **custom field** filters on every asset-based report, so you can ask for "Booking compliance for assets whose Condition is Good" or "Idle cameras of one model". Applied filters show as chips, the CSV and PDF exports carry exactly the same filters, and the filters are saved in the URL, so you can bookmark or share a specific report view.

![The Asset Distribution report with its filter bar (Category, Location, Asset model, Custom field) above four donut breakdowns, including the new By Asset Model](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/reports-filter-bar.webp)

### Read the Numbers

Reports combine **KPI cards** for headline numbers, **charts** for trends and distributions, and **tables** for the underlying records. Compliance and utilization reports use color to flag entries that need attention.

![Asset Distribution report showing donut breakdowns by category, location, and status](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/reports-distribution.webp)

![Booking Compliance report showing KPI cards, an on-time vs late chart, and the underlying booking table](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/reports-booking-compliance.webp)

### Values That Add Up

Shelf holds one value per unit, and every money figure in a report multiplies it by the quantity that figure is about: the units booked for value at risk, the units placed at a location for a location bucket, the units a custodian holds for a custody total. An asset split across two sites is counted at each one for the units it actually has there, not twice at full value, so **Asset Distribution** breaks the same total down four ways — by category, by location, by status and by asset model — and each breakdown adds back up to the headline.

**No Location** is the bucket for assets with no placement recorded anywhere, and it carries their full value. Stock that is *partly* placed is the one deliberate exception: an asset with six units at a location and four not yet put anywhere contributes only the six, and the remaining four are not folded into **No Location**. That bucket has to describe exactly the assets you get when you click it, which is the assets with no placement at all. So on a workspace that keeps partly-placed stock, the location breakdown can total less than the headline — by design, and only there. Category, status and asset model (assets without one sit in **No model**) always account for every unit.

For teams tracking cabling, batteries, sandbags or any pooled supply, that is the difference between a report you can hand to finance and one you have to rebuild in a spreadsheet first.

### Export the Result

Every report can be exported to **CSV** for further analysis in a spreadsheet or BI tool. Chart-based reports can additionally be downloaded as a **PDF** that captures the rendered visualization alongside the data — handy for board packs and customer reviews. Reports without a chart (Asset Inventory, Custody Snapshot, Overdue Items, Idle Assets, Top Booked Kits, Audit Completion) export to CSV.

The Asset Inventory and Custody Snapshot exports carry the arithmetic rather than only the result: quantity, unit value and total value as separate columns, in the CSV and totalled the same way in the PDF.

## Why It Matters

### Stop Reconstructing Data Manually

Before reports, answering "how many bookings were returned late last quarter?" meant exporting bookings to CSV and writing formulas. Now it is a report you can run in seconds and export with one click.

### Catch Problems Before They Compound

The Overdue Items and Booking Compliance reports surface the small leaks — late returns, ignored bookings, custody assignments that drag on — that turn into lost equipment.

### Justify Inventory Decisions

The Idle Assets and Asset Utilization reports show what is actually being used and what is not. Use them to defend equipment budgets, redistribute under-used assets between teams, or retire what nobody is booking anymore.

### Reduce Custodian Disputes

The Custody Snapshot report makes it impossible to argue about who has what — every asset currently in custody is listed with the assigned team member.

### Show That the Counting Got Done

The Audit Completion report turns a season of audits into one rate: how many were completed, which are overdue, and how many assets the completed ones found missing, with the accuracy of each count beside it. A quarterly audit program can be checked in one screen instead of audit by audit.

## Benefits

- **Self-serve** — Operations managers run reports themselves, without waiting on engineering or exporting to BI tools. Reports are an Administrator and Owner surface, included on the Plus and Team plans
- **Filterable** — Every booking, asset and custody report except Top Booked Kits can be filtered from a filter bar that matches how teams already organize their inventory
- **Shareable** — URLs encode the filters, so a saved view can be sent to a colleague or bookmarked
- **Exportable** — CSV for analysis, PDF for distribution
- **Live where it matters** — Snapshot reports (Custody, Overdue, Inventory) always reflect current state
