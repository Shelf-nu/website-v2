# Calendar

The calendar gives teams a visual overview of every reservation, custody event, and availability gap across their entire inventory. Instead of digging through lists or asking colleagues, anyone can open the calendar and see exactly what is booked, what is available, and what is overdue.

The calendar is the visual layer of Shelf's [equipment scheduling and reservation system](/solutions/equipment-reservations), which handles booking, conflict prevention, and custody in one place.

## What the Calendar Solves

Scheduling shared equipment without a visual timeline creates recurring problems:

- **Scheduling conflicts**: Teams unknowingly book the same asset for overlapping time slots because no one has a clear picture of what is already reserved.
- **Lack of visibility**: Managers cannot see at a glance which items are in use, which are sitting idle, and which are overdue for return.
- **Coordination gaps**: Departments working in silos create bottlenecks when they compete for the same pool of resources without a shared view.
- **Missed returns**: Without a visual indicator of overdue items, late returns go unnoticed until someone else needs the equipment.

Shelf's calendar view solves each of these by putting availability, reservations, and status into a single visual timeline that the whole team can access.

## How the Calendar Works in Shelf

### Month, Week, and Day Views

Switch between month, week, and day views depending on the level of detail you need. The month view works well for long-range planning. The week and day views are ideal for daily coordination and last-minute scheduling.

The calendar reads in each person's own settings. Weeks start on the day you chose (Monday, Sunday, or Saturday), times use your 12 or 24-hour preference, and bookings sit on your time zone's clock, so a distributed team looking at the same calendar each sees it laid out the way they expect. Set yours under [Account settings → General](/knowledge-base/date-time-and-timezone-preferences).

### Color-Coded Status

Each booking is color-coded to reflect its current status, so you can scan the calendar and understand the state of every reservation without opening one:

| Status | Colour |
|---|---|
| Reserved | Blue |
| Ongoing (checked out) | Purple |
| Overdue | Red |
| Complete | Green |
| Draft, Cancelled, Archived | Grey |

These are the same colours a booking wears everywhere else in Shelf: on the bookings list, on the booking itself, and on an asset's own bookings. A booking that reads as a problem in one view reads as a problem in all of them.

![Day view of the booking calendar with a multi-day booking shown as a full-height block](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/calendar-day.webp)

### Overdue Highlighting

Items that have not been returned by their scheduled end time turn red automatically. Equipment managers can spot overdue assets in seconds and follow up before the delay cascades into the next reservation.

---

### Integrated with Bookings and Custody

The calendar is not a standalone feature. It draws directly from the bookings and custody systems, so every reservation and handoff appears in real time. When a booking is created or a custody transfer happens, the calendar updates instantly.

See: [Bookings](/features/bookings) | [Custody](/features/custody)

### Export Bookings to Any Calendar App

Every booking can be exported as an `.ics` file and imported into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or any other RFC-compliant calendar application. Open any non-draft booking and click **Add to Calendar** to download the event file. The exported event includes the booking dates and a direct link back to the booking in Shelf.

![Add to Calendar button on a Shelf booking page](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/add-to-calendar-button.jpg)

This lets custodians keep their personal calendars aligned with their reservations without manual entry, and allows managers to share booking timelines with people outside of Shelf.

---

### Subscribe to a Live Booking Feed

Beyond one-off exports, each member can **subscribe** their calendar to a live feed of their Shelf bookings. Open the **Calendar** page and click **Subscribe** (or go to **Account Settings → Calendars**) to get a secret feed URL, then add it to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, or Outlook once. From then on, bookings appear alongside the member's other events and **stay up to date automatically** — new, moved, and cancelled bookings sync on the calendar app's own refresh, with no re-exporting.

The feed mirrors what the member can already see in Shelf: administrators who can view all bookings can publish a **shared team calendar** for everyone to subscribe to, while self-service and base members get a feed of just their own reservations. The URL is secret and can be **regenerated** or **revoked** at any time. Available free on all Team workspaces.

See: [Subscribe Your Calendar to Shelf Bookings](/knowledge-base/subscribe-your-calendar-to-shelf-bookings)

## Benefits

- **Prevent double bookings** with a clear visual timeline of all reservations
- **Maximize asset utilization** by identifying idle equipment and open time slots
- **Improve team coordination** with a shared, always-current view of availability
- **Catch overdue returns early** with automatic status highlighting
- **Reduce admin overhead** by eliminating back-and-forth about what is free
- **Sync with personal calendars** by exporting any booking as an `.ics` file, or **subscribe** to a live feed that keeps all your bookings updated in Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar automatically

## Common Use Cases

- **AV equipment rooms**: Coordinators see which cameras, projectors, and microphones are booked across the week and plan around availability.
- **Shared vehicles**: Fleet managers view vehicle reservations across the month and redistribute demand across the pool.
- **Lab equipment**: Researchers check the calendar before scheduling experiments to confirm instrument availability.
- **Construction tools**: Site managers coordinate tool assignments across crews and job sites using the day and week views.
