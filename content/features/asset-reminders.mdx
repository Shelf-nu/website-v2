# Asset Reminders

A reminder is a date on an asset with a message and a list of people. When the date arrives, Shelf emails those people and writes a note on the asset. That is the whole feature, and it covers a lot: inspections, calibrations, warranty ends, licence renewals, "decide whether to retire this".

![Asset reminders overview showing scheduled notifications](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/reminders2-min.png)

---

## What Reminders Solve

Dates that live in a spreadsheet, a calendar or someone's head slip. A reminder lives on the asset it is about, so:

- **Nothing is forgotten**: the inspection date is attached to the thing being inspected.
- **The right people hear**: each reminder names its own recipients, so the lab manager gets the calibration reminders and the IT lead gets the warranty ones.
- **There is a record**: every reminder that fires leaves a note on the asset's timeline.

---

## How Reminders Work in Shelf

### Set on the asset

Open an asset, choose **Actions > Set Reminder**, give it a name, a message, a date and time, and pick the team members who should receive it. Any team member with a Shelf login can be chosen, and one asset can carry as many reminders as you need.

### Emailed when the date arrives

At the chosen date and time Shelf sends one email to each person on the reminder, with the asset, your message and a link to it. If a recipient has since left the workspace, their email goes to the workspace owner instead, so no reminder is lost. Reminders on an archived asset are skipped.

![Asset reminder notification example](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/asset-reminder-notice.jpg)

### Seen before they fire

Upcoming reminders show in three places: the **Upcoming reminders** card on the Home page, the asset's own overview, and the **Reminders** page in the sidebar, which lists every reminder in the workspace with its status, **Pending** or **Sent**. Pending reminders can be edited; sent ones can only be deleted.

### What a reminder is not, yet

Each reminder is one date, set by hand. Reminders do not repeat on a schedule and they are not driven by a date custom field such as *Warranty expiry* or *Next calibration*: if you store those dates on your assets, you set the reminder yourself, 30 or 90 days ahead, as you prefer. Repeating and field-driven reminders are on our list, but not built. Return dates for borrowed equipment are handled by [Bookings](/features/bookings), which send their own reminders.

---

## Benefits

- **Deadlines attached to the asset**, not to a person's memory
- **Named recipients per reminder**, so each team hears only about its own gear
- **An audit trail**, since every fired reminder becomes a note on the asset
- **Workspace-wide overview** on the Reminders page and the Home card
