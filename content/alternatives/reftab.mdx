# Reftab Alternative

Reftab is a cloud-based IT asset management tool: a hardware and software register for IT teams, with check-in and checkout, reservations, request approvals and integrations into device-management tools. Shelf differs in four ways: open-source code you can self-host, flat pricing with no asset quota, separate workspaces per department, and kit checks on return.

Reftab details on this page were checked on reftab.com on 29 October 2026.

---

## Overview: Reftab vs Shelf

Reftab's free plan tracks up to 50 assets with no time limit. The paid Hardware Asset Management plan is listed at $125 per month with unlimited users, and the Complete ITAM bundle is custom-priced "based on assets + software users". Reftab's pricing FAQ refers to a "billable asset quota": paid plans are billed against the number of active assets. Reftab is proprietary SaaS: there is no self-hosted option and the source code is not public.

Shelf focuses on what happens when equipment moves between people. It tracks custody chains, reservations, and kit completeness, and every interaction (check-out, return, location update) can happen through a QR scan on any smartphone. Shelf is open source with unlimited assets on every plan, and unlimited users on the Team plan.

## Where Shelf Takes a Different Approach

### 1. Flat Pricing With No Asset Quota

Reftab's free plan stops at 50 assets. Beyond that, paid plans bill against an asset quota: the pricing FAQ talks about a "billable asset quota", and the ITAM bundle is priced "based on assets + software users". A price that climbs with the asset count is built for a fleet of laptops. It punishes a store room of cables, batteries and lenses.

Shelf does not cap assets on any plan, and the Team plan has no user cap. Whether you track 200 items or 20,000, the price stays the same. For teams whose inventory is thousands of cables, lenses, tools and batteries rather than a few hundred laptops, that difference decides the bill.

See: [Pricing](/pricing)

---

### 2. Open Source and Self-Hosting

Reftab is proprietary SaaS. The source code is not available, there is no self-hosted option, and your data lives on Reftab's infrastructure. For organizations with data sovereignty requirements, compliance mandates, or a policy of inspectable code, that is a hard constraint.

Shelf is [open source](/solutions/open-source-asset-management) with a fully public codebase. Teams can read every line, self-host on their own infrastructure, or use Shelf's managed cloud. There is no vendor lock-in, and teams with development resources can extend Shelf to connect with any system their organization uses.

---

### 3. Multi-Department Workspaces

Reftab runs one account per organization, scoped with locations, sub-locations and access roles. That is enough for one IT team managing one estate. Separate tenants per department exist, but they are an upgrade you request from Reftab's support, not something a department sets up itself.

Shelf uses [workspaces](/features/workspaces) to give each team a fully separate inventory. Each workspace has its own assets, users, bookings and settings, and a person can belong to several. Nobody sees another department's equipment unless they are invited into that workspace.

See: [Workspaces](/features/workspaces)

### 4. Kit Checks on Return

Reftab has kits and kit templates: a template lists the required components and scanning fills in the checklist when a kit is assembled. Reserving a kit template is limited to admins, per Reftab's own documentation, so the people who actually use the gear cannot book one from a template.

Shelf's [kits](/features/kits) are a fixed set of named assets that anyone with the right role books, checks out and returns together. When a kit comes back, Shelf flags which components are missing before the next person picks it up. If your pain is the slow loss of chargers and lens caps from shared kits, that return check is the feature to compare.

See: [Kits](/features/kits)

![Kits overview showing grouped equipment sets](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/kits.jpg)

### 5. Bookings That Carry Into Check-Out

Both tools take reservations. Reftab's user portal lets people reserve items themselves, with admin-set rules (buffer times, booking windows, business hours) and optional approvals. With the portal's "Reserve Only" setting, staff check the items out at pickup.

Shelf's [booking system](/features/bookings) (Team plan) works from a live availability calendar, prevents overlapping bookings automatically, and carries the booking straight into check-out and return by QR scan on a phone, kit included.

See: [Bookings](/features/bookings)

![Bookings overview showing reservation calendar](https://qliecghuzfchfjwaisyx.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/website-images/features/bookings.jpg)

---

### 6. Custody History

Reftab logs loans (who, out, back, notes) and keeps a field changelog per asset.

Shelf keeps a [custody chain](/features/custody) for every asset, with each check-out, return and transfer stamped with the person and the time. The record can be written at the hand-over itself: scan the asset's QR code on a phone and assign custody from there.

## When Teams Choose Shelf Instead of Reftab

- **The inventory is large and cheap per item**: Thousands of cables, tools, props or batteries make per-asset pricing expensive, and Shelf has no asset quota
- **Open source is a requirement**: Data sovereignty, self-hosting, or a policy of inspectable code
- **Several departments share one platform**: Each team wants its own workspace, members and settings rather than one shared account scoped by roles
- **Kits must come back complete**: Return verification that flags missing components is the daily problem

---

## When Reftab May Be a Better Fit

- **Software licences in the same register**: Reftab tracks software and SaaS users alongside hardware. Shelf has no software or licence tracking
- **IT integrations**: Reftab connects to Intune, Jamf, Okta, Jira, Zendesk and more on paid plans. Shelf's integration path is its open-source codebase and CSV import/export
- **Help-desk workflow**: Request approvals and work orders, for IT departments that gate equipment behind a sign-off

Both platforms track physical assets, but they optimize for different realities. Reftab is built around an IT register of hardware and software. Shelf is built for teams where equipment circulates daily between many people.
