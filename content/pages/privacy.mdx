## Introduction
At Shelf.nu, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, process, and store your personal data, and your rights regarding that data.
As an asset management platform, we maintain a careful balance between data privacy and essential business record-keeping.

## Data Controller
The data controller for Shelf.nu is Shelf Asset Management, Inc. The company is responsible for ensuring that your personal data is processed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws.

## Data Collection
We collect several types of personal data, including:
- Names
- Email addresses
- Account identifier (your user ID)
- Photos and images you attach to your data (asset photos, audit-evidence photos, profile pictures)
- Team Member Names
- Asset custody records
- Equipment check-out / check-in history
- Booking records
- Asset modification logs
- Notes and other content you add to records
- Scan records, including the time of the scan and, where the device or browser provides it and you have allowed it, the location at which the scan was made

We do not use any of this data for tracking across apps or websites, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Purpose of Data Collection
We collect this data to:
- Identify you as a user and grant access to our cloud product
- Communicate with you and provide customer support
- Display your profile picture next to your name on our platform
- Associate team members with your account
- Maintain accurate business records of asset custody and usage
- Enable organizations to track their equipment effectively
- Show an asset's last known location to the organization that owns it
- Provide audit trails for asset management
- Support legitimate business documentation needs

## Legal Basis for Processing
Our data processing is based on:
- Contractual necessity (providing asset management services)
- Legitimate business purposes (maintaining asset records)
- Legal obligations (supporting audit and accounting requirements)
- User consent (for optional features like profile pictures)

## Data Processors
We use Supabase as our database provider, which processes and stores your personal data on our behalf. Supabase has implemented adequate security measures to protect your personal data and prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, modification, or destruction.
Shelf has carefully reviewed Supabase's Data Processing Addendum and is satisfactory for our use case.

The following service providers process personal data as part of running Shelf (app.shelf.nu and the Shelf Companion mobile app):
- **Supabase**: database, sign-in accounts and file storage, in AWS region eu-central-1 (Frankfurt, Germany)
- **Fly.io**: application servers, in Amsterdam, the Netherlands
- **Cloudflare**: the edge network that all traffic to app.shelf.nu passes through, and page-view analytics
- **Resend, sending through Amazon SES**: delivery of sign-in codes, invitations and notifications (recipient address and message content), in the United States
- **Stripe**: billing. Card details are entered on Stripe's own pages and never reach Shelf; Stripe receives the billing contact's name and email address
- **Sentry**: error monitoring, in the United States. Error reports can include user and workspace identifiers
- **Microsoft Clarity**: page-usage recordings and heatmaps of the web application
- **Crisp**: support chat. When you open the in-app chat, your name and email address are shared with Crisp
- **PostHog**: product analytics (sign-up, upgrade and cancellation events, keyed by an internal user ID)
- **MapTiler**: map images on location pages
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim**: turns location addresses into map coordinates (address text only)

## Data Retention
For active accounts, we retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy. For deleted accounts:
- Essential business records (asset custody, check-out history, booking records) are retained to meet business and audit requirements
- Personal profile data is deleted upon account closure
- Transaction logs necessary for asset tracking are maintained as required by law and legitimate business needs

## Data Security
We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure that your personal data is secure and protected from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction. Our security measures include:
- Data stored in the EU: database and files in Frankfurt, Germany; application servers in Amsterdam, the Netherlands
- TLS 1.3 encryption for data in transit
- AES-256 encryption for stored data
- Strict access controls
Our data processor, Supabase, also implements robust security measures to protect your data.

## Your Rights and Practical Implementation
You have certain rights regarding your personal data, including the right to:
- Access your data through your profile and activity history
- Correct your personal information
- Delete optional personal data (profile pictures, optional fields)
- Object to data processing where applicable
Please note that certain data, such as asset custody records and equipment check-out history, must be maintained for legitimate business purposes and cannot be deleted as they constitute essential business records.

## European Users and GDPR
For our European users, we align with GDPR requirements while maintaining necessary business records. While we respect data privacy rights including the "right to be forgotten," it's important to understand that much of our data processing falls under legitimate business and legal requirements that override erasure rights under GDPR Article 17, specifically:
Asset custody records, check-out histories, and booking logs must be maintained to:
- Comply with legal obligations (accounting, auditing)
- Establish chain of custody for legal claims if needed
- Document proper asset handling and business operations
We store this data in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany, and our application servers process it in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. Some of the service providers listed under Data Processors, such as email delivery and error monitoring, operate in the United States. Personal data not essential for business records (like profile pictures or optional information) can be deleted upon request.

## Mobile Application

Shelf provides a companion mobile app for iOS and Android (Shelf Companion) that connects to your existing Shelf workspace for field operations such as QR/barcode scanning, asset lookups, custody handoffs, and live audits. The mobile app communicates with the same Shelf API as the web platform — there is no separate backend. The following disclosures apply specifically to the mobile app:

**Camera access** — The app requests access to your device camera for two purposes: (1) live QR code and barcode scanning to identify assets — this camera feed is processed on the device and is not itself recorded or stored; and (2) optionally capturing an asset's main image when you update it from the asset detail screen. Photos you capture or attach this way are uploaded to and stored by Shelf via the same Shelf API as the web platform, linked to your account, and used only to provide the service. They are not used for tracking or advertising.

**On-device storage** — Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using the platform's secure storage (the iOS Keychain, and the Android Keystore-backed equivalent, via Expo SecureStore). These tokens are cleared when you sign out.

**Location access** — The app can attach the device's location to a scan, so that an asset's record shows where it was last seen. This matches the behaviour of the web app, which already records the browser's location on a scan.

- **It is optional.** The app asks for location permission once, the first time you open the scanner. If you decline, scanning and every other feature continue to work normally; scans simply carry no coordinates. You can change the permission at any time in your device settings.
- **Foreground only.** The app requests "while using the app" location. It does not track your device in the background, and it does not follow assets continuously between scans.
- **What is stored.** When permission is granted, the latitude and longitude at the time of the scan are sent to the same Shelf API as the web platform and stored on the resulting scan record, alongside the existing scan details. A position older than five minutes is discarded rather than attached.
- **What it is used for.** Showing your team an asset's last known location. Location is never used for advertising, and it is not shared with third parties or used to track you across other apps or websites.

**Other data collection** — Beyond the camera, photo and location access described above, the mobile app does not collect device identifiers, analytics, telemetry, or crash reports beyond what the web platform already describes in this policy. All data flows through the same Shelf API documented above.

**Children's privacy** — The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.
